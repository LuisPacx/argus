# 20: Chain-sourced creates (fix the PumpPortal coverage gap)
**Model:** sonnet / high — the sampling must be unbiased and the credit cap must hold; both are easy to get subtly wrong.

**What to build:** PumpPortal's free `subscribeNewToken` misses about 47% of pump.fun creates, mostly the newer `create_v2` shapes (mayhem, cashback flag, BuyV3 or wrapper-paired creates: 88-100% missed; older shapes 1-5%). The Study's population is therefore biased toward old-shape creates, and Phase 4 (which compares migrators with a comparison population) depends on that population being unbiased. The Operator approved fixing it by reading creates from chain, read-only, through Helius. PumpPortal stays the live feed and the migration signal.
Evidence: `.scratch/phase-3-feedback/research/create-coverage-2026-11-11.md` (incl. its "Helius check" section: 264 of 496 creates seen in one 15-minute window) and `.scratch/phase-3-feedback/research/dune-2026-11-11.md`.

Two jobs, one module:

1. **Migrated creates.** For every migration PumpPortal reports (about 800/day), find that mint's create transaction on chain (the earliest successful transaction touching the mint; the create instruction is the pump-program instruction whose `accounts[1]` is the mint-authority PDA `TSLvdd1pWpHVjahSpsvCXUbgwsL3JAcvokwaKt1eokM`), read it with `getTransaction`, and fill the Study create fields and the metadata `uri` that PumpPortal never delivered. Mints PumpPortal already delivered are left alone unless a field is empty.
2. **Comparison sample.** Once a day, list the previous UTC day's successful creates from the mint-authority PDA (`getSignaturesForAddress`, 1,000 per page, failed transactions dropped), draw a uniform random sample (default 2,000, config), read each with `getTransaction`, and store them as the comparison population with their sampling weight. Weight = creates listed that day / creates sampled, stored per row with the day, so Phase 4 can weight back to the full population. Sampled mints that also migrated are kept (the labeller, ticket 03, decides the label); the sample is drawn from all creates, never from "non-migrated" ones, because migration is not known at draw time and filtering on it would bias the sample.

**Blocked by:** 18 ADR 0002 test allows read-only RPC (branch `adr0002-test`; the Helius client is the single module `packages/core/src/study/helius.ts`)

Status: done

**Chain access rule (ticket 18):** every chain call goes through `packages/core/src/study/helius.ts`, per-method wrappers only (`getSignaturesForAddress`, `getTransaction`, and, if used, `getTransactionsForAddress`), against the pinned URL `https://mainnet.helius-rpc.com/`. No new file may name the host or an RPC method, and no generic `rpc(method, params)` is exported. This ticket adds wrappers to that module and puts the sampling and parsing logic elsewhere under `packages/core/src/study/`, calling the wrappers. Widening the allowed methods needs the Operator's say-so.

**Credit budget (cited, checked 2026-11-11):**
- Helius Free plan: 1M credits per month, 10 RPC requests per second, no daily limit listed (https://www.helius.dev/pricing). Overage behaviour on Free is not documented there; treat the monthly figure as a hard wall.
- Costs (https://www.helius.dev/docs/billing/credits): `getTransaction` 1 credit, `getSignaturesForAddress` 1 credit, `getTransactionsForAddress` 10 credits minimum (signatures-only flat 10; full transactions 10 per 100 returned).
- Estimate: sample listing ~50 pages (~47k creates/day) + 2,000 reads = ~2,050/day; migrated creates 800 x (1-10 to locate + 1 to read) = about 1,600 to 8,800/day depending on how the earliest signature is found. About 4,000 to 11,000/day, or 120k to 340k/month.
- Default daily cap `chainCreatesDailyCreditCap` = 25,000 credits (750k/month at 30 days), leaving about 250k/month for tickets 03, 04 and 10, which use the same key. If those tickets measure a larger need, the Operator lowers the sample size before raising the cap. Raising the cap above 33,000/day is a reviewed code change, since it breaks the month.
- Sample size `chainCreatesSampleSize` default 2,000/day, config.
- Check the Free figures again at implementation time; if Helius changed them, stop and tell the Operator.

**Open design point for the implementer to settle with a recorded fixture, not a guess:** finding a migrated mint's earliest transaction. `getSignaturesForAddress` on a busy mint needs many pages back to the oldest (1 credit each, but unbounded); `getTransactionsForAddress` with ascending order and limit 1 is 10 credits but bounded. Pick by measured credits on real migrated mints; record the choice in the ticket's Comments.

**Interplay:**
- 02 (snapshots): Snapshots are taken at a fixed age after create. A chain-sourced create found after the fact cannot get a Snapshot at that age, so chain-sourced rows carry create fields and uri only and are marked `source` = `chain_migrated` or `chain_sample`; ticket 02's snapshot job must skip them rather than take a late Snapshot labelled as fixed-age. Whether Phase 4 needs fixed-age Snapshots of the comparison sample is an Operator decision (it would need the sample drawn live, in hourly batches, instead of daily); this ticket records the question and does not decide it.
- 03 (chain labeller): sampled and migrated rows are labelled by the same 24h/7d job; this ticket adds rows, never labels. Both share one credit budget, so credits are logged per job and per day in one place.
- 19 (IPFS gateway fix, written separately): filling `uri` is only useful if metadata can be fetched; 19 owns fetching and gateways, this ticket only fills `uri`. No fetching here.
- 10 (own-launch tracking) and 04 (bot filter): also read chain through `helius.ts`; the daily cap here is separate from any run cap they have, and the monthly total is the thing to watch.
- Phase 4 depends on this population being unbiased: do not drop, dedupe away or re-weight rows beyond the stored weight.

## Comments

- New evidence for the gap (2026-11-19): `.scratch/research/petal-dev/onchain.md` (section 10 and "Data coverage"): argus's PumpPortal feed carried 9 of one mass-launcher's 1,993 tokens (Nov 15-18) and none of his 32 migrated ones, most of them `create_v2` and about 58% quoted in a non-SOL asset (tokenized stocks), the shapes this ticket reads from chain. Its "Data coverage" section says `getTransactionsForAddress` (full history, time filters) worked on the Helius key; this ticket's measurements confirm it, and also use `getSignaturesForAddress` and `getTransaction`.
- Design point settled (2026-11-19, 12 real migrated mints, the newest migrations in the Study table spread across the list; `packages/core/src/fixtures/helius-earliest-tx-measurement.json`): finding a migrated mint's earliest transaction by paging `getSignaturesForAddress` back to the oldest took 2, 4, 5, 5, 9, 10, 10, 12, 13, 14 pages and two mints passed the 25-page limit of the experiment (25,000 signatures seen and still going), plus 1 credit to read the create: 3 to 26+ credits each, 146 or more for the 12 against 120. `getTransactionsForAddress` ascending, limit 1, full transactions, status succeeded cost 10 credits for every mint and returned the create itself (no second read); its first transaction matched the create signature PumpPortal had recorded for all 12. Choice: the history call (`ChainReader.earliestTransaction`): 10 credits bounded, about 8,000 a day at 800 migrations a day, against paging that is cheaper on 4 of 12 (by 4 to 7 credits), equal on 1, dearer on 7 and unbounded on 2. A cheaper hybrid (page up to N then fall back) was not built: the mean gain is small and a bounded worst case is what the daily cap needs.
- Free plan figures re-checked 2026-11-19 (helius.dev pricing and billing docs): 1M credits a month, 10 RPC requests a second, no daily limit listed, overage not documented; costs unchanged (`getTransaction` 1, `getSignaturesForAddress` 1, `getTransactionsForAddress` 10 flat signatures-only or 10 per 100 full, minimum 10). The billing page itself states no request rate; the pricing page's 10 a second is what the limiter uses. Nothing changed, so the ticket ran as written.
- Daily budget with the measured cost: sample listing about 50 pages (a 15-minute window held 496 creates, about 47k a day) + 2,000 reads = about 2,050; migrated about 800 x 10 = 8,000; about 10,000 a day, 300k a month, inside the 25,000 cap and leaving room for tickets 03, 04 and 10. First run on an existing database also works off the backlog of migrated mints without a create (about 300 mints a run, 3,000 credits, until the cap).
- Credits spent by this work (counted in code, one key): about 660 in total for the measurement (254), harvesting the fixtures (about 390: 800 recent creates and 90 old ones as 100-per-10 history pages, one failed signature read, the 2025 windows) and the history fixture (10). Free plan month total is far from 1M.
- Real finding: transaction version 1 exists on mainnet (a history page failed with "Transaction version (1) is not supported" until `maxSupportedTransactionVersion` was raised from 0 to 1). `helius.ts` sends 1 on both reads (`MAX_TX_VERSION`); a future version 2 would fail a whole page the same way, so a new "not supported" error is worth a look when it appears in the run log.
- Create_v2 argument forms in the wild (2026-11-10 14:00 window, 170 creates): 11 trailing bytes (mayhem, cashback, fee bps u64, holder reward) is the IDL's; 10 bytes (no holder flag), 2 bytes and 1 byte are older program versions; 11 creates had a 3-byte tail the pinned IDL does not describe. The parser reads only what the IDL describes (see the create-parsing box); `ParsedCreate.trailingBytes` is kept so an unknown form is visible. Not one cashback-enabled create in about 3,000 read; the research note's "trailing 1 = cashback" turned out to be other bytes. The holder-reward flag (`is_holder_reward`) is new to this repo and is read but not stored.
- Choices made where the ticket was open: (1) `creator` is the signing user of the create (what PumpPortal's `traderPublicKey` reports), the `creator` argument (the fee beneficiary, which can be a per-token address) is parsed but not stored. (2) Weight = successful signatures listed that day / signatures drawn; a drawn signature that is not a create (a pool creation, about 0.4%) is skipped, so the stored rows' weights sum to the day's creates and a little under the listed total; `study_chain_days.skipped` says how many. (3) A day is listed again if nothing was listed for it (an empty day is asked for at each run for two days; in production a day always has about 47k). The sample owes yesterday and, if never drawn, the day before. (4) A failed listing is not retried for 30 minutes (state in the worker). (5) The sample draws from signatures, so a mint that is in the sample and migrated later (before or after the draw) is one row whichever path found it first.
- Operator question, recorded and not decided (ticket text): does Phase 4 need fixed-age Snapshots of the comparison sample? It would need the sample drawn live in hourly batches instead of daily, with a Snapshot at 60 s. Chain-sourced rows have create fields and uri only and are never due for a Snapshot; Phase 4's comparison on uri-derived features (metadata, website) needs ticket 19's fetching for these rows, and on anything older than the capture window cannot use a fixed-age Snapshot.
- Deployment notes (nothing deployed or restarted by this ticket): the job is on only when `HELIUS_API_KEY` is in the worker's environment, and `pnpm dev:start` copied only `ARGUS_*` keys from the repo-root `.env` into the worker (`scripts/dev-env.mjs`), so a key that lived in `.env` as `HELIUS_API_KEY` did not reach it. The Operator approved (2026-11-19) allowlisting that one name: `dev-env.mjs` now passes `ARGUS_*` keys and, by exact match, `HELIUS_API_KEY` (tested: other non-ARGUS keys such as `SOLANA_RPC_URL`, `WALLET_*`, `PRIVATE_KEY` and look-alike names still do not pass; the shell still wins; the start line names keys, never values). Takes effect at the next `pnpm dev:start`. Without the key the worker logs "chain creates off". Requests are limited per process at 8 a second; ticket 03, 04 and 10 jobs on the same key need their own share of the 10.
- Recording fixtures (2026-11-19): read-only calls only, key read from the environment in a throwaway script outside the repo and never printed or written; the fixtures hold no key (`phase3-fixtures.test.ts` checks `api-key=` and UUIDs). The Study table copy used to pick migrated mints was a `.backup` of the live database under `~/.cache/argus-scratch/`, deleted afterwards.
