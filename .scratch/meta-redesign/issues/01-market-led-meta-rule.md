# 01: A Meta is led by a token's market cap (rule, kept market cap, ADR 0014, GLOSSARY, config, pipeline graph)
**Model:** sonnet / high. The core rule of the redesign: a new pure module with hysteresis state, a new stored table, a change to the Meta tick and its DexScreener budget, one ADR that supersedes part of another, and config in six places. Every later ticket reads what this one stores, so a wrong shape here is expensive.

**What to build:** replace "a Meta is a launch theme that ranks high on creator counts" with "a Meta is a current trend with **a token really high** (millions of market cap), lasting minutes to days". Plain code, no new model call. The Meta tick finds each **leader** token (any age) by its market cap, matches it to the launch theme that copies it, and stores at most 3 **Metas** and up to 3 **Watching** subjects. ## Why (evidence, so the thresholds are not argued again)

- The user's complaint: about 50 Metas on the page, many with one token; a Meta is 2 or 3 current trends and a token at millions of market cap (the NVIDIA CEO case). No revert was ever made; the cause is the definition: `/meta` asks for 50 rows (`meta.vue:9`, `META_RULE.maxRowsPerWindow` 50, `config.ts:81`) and ranks by creators first (`config.ts:76`), with market cap only a late tie-break fetched for the top 10 groups (`compute.ts:195-217`).
- Run 143: 24 h window 50 rows, none at or above $250k, the best a Jensen group at $93k. Live 15:35Z: `Jensen Huang ($JENSEN)` `B2YpP...pump` $96k (created 11-14, a day before the Jensen group's window, so no Meta saw it), `Stand with Jensen` $95k, ASTRALIS $1.7k. Over the stored history, 24 of 143 runs had a row at or above $1M, so "No Meta right now" will be the normal state most of the time. That is intended; ticket 02 designs for it.
- DexScreener's trending feed already names the big tokens, but the Source drops the figure: `marketCapUsd` and `fdvUsd` are parsed (`sources/dexscreener.ts:116-129`) and thrown away when the Signal is built (`dexscreener.ts:166-176`, `summarize` at `:189`).

## The rule (all numbers are config, default shown)

- **Subject and leader.** A subject is a launch theme group (`meta/group.ts`, `meta/theme.ts`, key from `meta/key.ts` `subjectKey`). Its **leader** is the token of any age with the highest current market cap that carries the subject, found two ways: (a) Solana tokens in the `dexscreener:trending` Signals, (b) the biggest token of each top launch theme (the `caps` the existing lookup already gathers for the top `dexGroups` groups, `compute.ts:197-217`). A token matches a subject when one of its `subjectKey` forms equals the group's key or one of its tokens' whole forms (`tokenWholeForms`, `theme.ts:73`, as `prompt-block.ts` `rowForms` does), never through a generic or too-short key (`isGenericKey`, `group.ts:39`; `newsMinKeyChars` 4). No RegExp built from a name (worker rules).
- **Copies.** The subject's distinct creators in the last 24 h (`counts["24h"].creators` of its group) must be at least **10** (`ARGUS_META_MIN_COPIES`). A leader with no group, or a group below 10, is never a Meta or Watching.
- **Becomes a Meta** when the leader's market cap is at least **$1,000,000** (`ARGUS_META_ENTER_MCAP_USD`) and copies hold. **Stays a Meta** while the leader is at least **$500,000** (`ARGUS_META_STAY_MCAP_USD`); a Meta between the two is `holding` (ticket 02 shows it as cooling). At most **3** Metas (`ARGUS_META_MAX_METAS`), highest leader cap first, ties by key.
- **Watching** (`ARGUS_META_WATCHING`, 3): the top 3 other subjects by leader cap that are below $1M and meet the copies rule. Not a Meta, labelled so everywhere. A Meta that falls below $500k is no longer a Meta and competes for Watching like any subject.
- **Caps are looked up every meta tick** within a call budget (`ARGUS_META_LEADER_CALLS`, default 4; one `tokenPairs` call takes up to 30 addresses, `compute.ts:20-22`, `dexscreener.ts:89`). Order of the lookup: the previous Metas' and Watching leaders first (so a Meta is never lost to the budget), then trending candidates by the figure stored on their Signal, then the rest. A lookup that fails or is over budget leaves that subject's status and last cap as they were (marked stale); it never drops a Meta and never creates one. State older than 2 h (a pause) is not carried (`ARGUS_META_STATE_MAX_MINUTES`, 120).

## What is there today (read 2026-11-15)

- Recompute: `recomputeMetas` (`compute.ts:148`) reads 24 h of creates, groups, counts, applies the size floor (`eligible`, `:195`), looks up caps for the top groups (`:197-217`), ranks (`compareStats`, `:92`), and writes one run (`saveRun`, `:251`; `store.ts:257`). The worker calls it every 15 min (`apps/worker/src/main.ts:337-345`). The DexScreener client is `metaDex` (`main.ts:336`).
- Stored shape: `meta_runs`, `meta_rows` (PK run, window, rank), `meta_summaries`, `meta_hot_events` (`meta/store.ts:113-167`). `MetaRow` has counts, top tokens, `marketCapUsd` (sum of the top 3) and `topMarketCapUsd`.
- A Source Signal has no structured field; `dexscreener:trending` Signals are `INSERT OR IGNORE` by id `dexscreener:trending:solana:<mint>` (`datastore.ts:1123`), so the figure is frozen at first sight and `published_at` is the pair's creation time. **Consequence:** a Signal says "this token was trending when first seen", not its cap now. It is a candidate list, not a price. The current cap always comes from the lookup. A token that stays high for days stays a candidate only through the carry-over of last tick's leaders. There is a usable index (`signals_source_ingested`, `datastore.ts:426`), so candidates are read by `ingested_at` in the last 24 h.

## Approach (a default; say if you want it changed)

1. **Keep the figure on the Signal.** `dexscreener.ts` `summarize` adds `market cap $N` (and `FDV $N` when different) to the Signal summary text, in the existing "Boosted on DexScreener. 24h volume ..." sentence; the DexPair fields stay. The summary feeds Narrative embedding, so keep it one short clause and re-run the dexscreener and ingest tests. A pure `trendingCandidates` reader (new `Datastore` read: Signals of source `dexscreener:trending`, `ingested_at >= since`, mint from the id, name/ticker from the title, cap parsed from the summary) returns `{mint, name, symbol, storedCapUsd?}`. Parsing is by string search, no dynamic RegExp.
2. **New module `packages/core/src/meta/leaders.ts`**, pure: `currentMetas({ groups, caps, candidates, previous, config })` returns `{ metas, watching }`, each row `{ key, label, leader: { mint, name, symbol, marketCapUsd, createdAt?, source: "trending" | "theme", stale }, holding, enteredAt, counts, topTokens }`. `enteredAt` is carried from the previous run while the subject stays a Meta. `counts` is the group's 6 h and 24 h `MetaCounts`, so a Meta carries the same numbers as a launch-theme row and never depends on that subject being inside the 50 stored rows. The subject's `key` is the group key, so summaries (`meta_summaries`), matching news (`meta/news.ts`) and meme Proposals (`meta_key`) keep working with no change of key.
3. **Compute.** After the existing cap lookup, `recomputeMetas` builds the candidate list, looks up the leaders' current caps (one more budgeted client call, `MetaDexClient.tokenPairs`, already the only DexScreener method it needs), calls `currentMetas`, and passes the result to `saveRun`. `RecomputeResult` gains `metas` and `watching` counts; the worker log line prints them and the leader of each Meta.
4. **Store.** New table `meta_current` (additive, `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` in `migrateMeta`, `store.ts:113`): run id, kind (`meta` or `watching`), rank, key, label, leader mint/name/symbol/cap/created/source/stale, holding, entered_at, counts and top tokens as JSON. Pruned with the runs (`prune`, `store.ts:288-295`). Reads: `Datastore.currentMetas()` (newest run's rows, a `MetaCurrent` with `metas`, `watching`, `computedAt`) and the previous run's rows for the carry. Rehearse the migration on a `.backup` copy and delete it.
5. **Config.** `MetaConfig` (`config.ts:25-75`) and `META_RULE` (`:77-100`) get `enterMcapUsd`, `stayMcapUsd`, `minCopies`, `maxMetas`, `watching`, `leaderCalls`, `stateMaxMinutes`; `metaConfigFromEnv` (`:142`) reads the `ARGUS_META_*` names above with the existing `intIn` bounds (stay must not exceed enter: a bad pair keeps the defaults). `.env.example:34-50` documents them, `startup-config.ts:64-66` adds a `meta_mcap=enter/stay` and `meta_copies=` token to the `[config]` line.
6. **Docs.** New **ADR 0014 "A Meta is led by a token's market cap"** (status accepted, 2026-11-15, decided by the Operator): the definition, the thresholds and why (the evidence above), the hysteresis, Watching, and that it **supersedes ADR 0012's ranking part** (the rank order decision and the 2026-11-11 list of what a Meta is). ADR 0012 gets a status note and keeps grouping, size floor, coverage, summaries and the prompt block text as history, each marked with the ticket that changes it. **GLOSSARY.md**: rewrite **Meta** in the user's words (a current trend with a token really high; minutes to days; at most 3), add **Watching**, rename **Meta ranking** to the launch-theme list ("All launch themes", counts only), leave **Hot now** for ticket 03. `docs/architecture.md` through `pnpm docs:graph` (do not hand-edit generated parts) and the hand-written Meta paragraphs at `:90`, `:263`, `:265`.
7. **Pipeline graph.** `packages/core/src/pipeline-graph.ts` Meta node (`:330-362`): rows for the new knobs (`live:` read from `metaConfigFromEnv` where the existing rows do), the `what` text, and a new edge from the DexScreener trending Source to the Meta node ("trending tokens and their market caps", beside `sources-meta`, `:423`). Existing Hot now rows stay until ticket 03.

## Open questions (defaults apply unless the user says otherwise)

1. **Does the copies rule apply to staying?** Default: no. A Meta stays on the leader's cap alone ($500k), as the user worded it. Effect: a Meta can outlive its launch wave while the token holds. Say if staying also needs 10 copies.
2. **A Watching floor.** Default: none (top 3 by cap below $1M that meet the copies rule). Effect: if only low-cap subjects exist, Watching shows a $2k token. Say if you want a floor (for example $50k).
3. **Candidate list freeze.** Because a trending Signal is stored once, a token the feed first showed 3 days ago is a candidate only if it was a leader last tick. Default: accept (carry-over covers a token that was ever a leader). Alternative: poll the trending feed live in the Meta tick (one more call per tick). Say if you want it.
