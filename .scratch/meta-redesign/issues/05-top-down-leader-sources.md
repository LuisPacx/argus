# 05: Top-down leaders: Jupiter and GeckoTerminal trending, the fake-token filter, stock exclusion, a call budget, DexScreener only to verify, bonk launches counted as copiers
**Model:** sonnet / high. Two new network clients over untrusted JSON, a filter whose thresholds decide what can lead a Meta, a hard call budget with a rate-limit window, a change to the ADR 0002 tripwire, a change to how the Study is read (bonk), and a rewire of the leader lookup in the Meta tick. A wrong filter lets a $12B fake lead a Meta; a wrong budget gets argus rate-limited.

**What to build:** the leaders of the Meta rule are found from what is trending now, not from the launch themes and not from DexScreener's paid boosts. Each Meta tick reads Jupiter's `toptrending/1h` and GeckoTerminal's Solana `trending_pools`, merges them per token, drops fakes and tokenized stocks, and hands the survivors to the existing leader lookup, where DexScreener verifies the current cap. A subject's copiers (10 or more distinct creators in 24 h) now count bonk launches as well as pump.fun ones. After this ticket the GTA VI case works end to end under its old name ("Lucia From GTA 6"); ticket 06 fixes the name and the grouping. Decided by the user on 2026-11-16 (`.scratch/cos.md`: "GTA DIAGNOSIS", "GTA USER" Q2, Q3; "RESEARCH top-down"; "USER: all as recommended" Q6, Q7, Q8; the design and its reasons are `docs/adr/0015-metas-are-found-top-down.md`, decisions 1 to 3). The raw facts (endpoints, fields, limits, ranks) are in `.scratch/meta-redesign/research-top-down-sources.md`.

**Status:** done

**Blocked by:** 04 (it reuses `retryingFetchText` for both new clients; a client that did not retry would fail the first transient error all over again). The Operator decided open question 1 on 2026-11-16 (Q12 a), so the Jupiter client is in scope.

## What is there today (read 2026-11-16)

- Leader lookup: `lookUpLeaders` (`meta/compute.ts:305`) builds its order from the previous Metas' and Watching leaders, then `trendingCandidates` (`meta/candidates.ts`, the `dexscreener:trending` Signals of the last 24 h by `ingested_at`, read back with `store.meta.trendingSignals`), at most `leaderCalls` (4) `tokenPairs` calls of 30 mints. It keeps the best cap per mint across pairs (`Math.max`) and does not look at liquidity or volume. `currentMetas` (`meta/leaders.ts`) then matches a leader to the subject that names it (`subjectKey` forms, never generic or under 4 characters) and applies the copies rule from `counts["24h"].creators`.
- The DexScreener Source builds Signals from `token-boosts/top/v1`, which is paid promotion. The GTA VI leader was one Signal on 11-07 and then gone from the 24 h read (ADR 0015, why).
- The Study: `createdBetween` (`study/store.ts:420`) leaves out `status = 'out-of-scope'`. `recordTokenEvent` (`:330`) stores a create whose pool is not `pump` as `out-of-scope` ("not a pump.fun launch (pool bonk)") but with its `name`, `symbol`, `creator`, `pool` and `created_at` filled, so bonk launches are in the table and no Meta read sees them. `compute.test.ts:176` pins that bonk creates are not in the base.
- Egress: `adr-0002.test.ts` bans the host `jup.ag` (`BANNED_HOSTS`, `(^|\.)jup\.ag$`) and the package `@jup-ag/*`, because Jupiter is a swap host; its header says the only exception is the Helius module and "widening any of these three needs the Operator's say-so". GeckoTerminal (`api.geckoterminal.com`) is not banned.
- Worker: the Meta tick is `recomputeMetas(store, { dex: metaDex, config, log })` every `ARGUS_META_INTERVAL_MINUTES` (15) (`apps/worker/src/main.ts:336-345`); clients get URL overrides from env for tests (`ARGUS_DEXSCREENER_SEARCH_URL`).
- Live facts (research, 2026-11-16): GTA VI `4kjruq...` is rank 13 of 50 on Jupiter `toptrending/1h` (mcap $7.04M, liquidity $272k, organic score 73) and rank 10 of 20 on GeckoTerminal `trending_pools` (fdv $7.08M, one pool of several, pool liquidity $68k, 24 h volume $720k). The fake `EcLbZ6...` "GTA VI" is $12.07B cap on $48.3M liquidity with $2.28 of 24 h volume. The TTWO/GTA6 pool (`TTWofwAg...`) is Take-Two's tokenized stock, FDV $4.6M. GeckoTerminal: 20 pools a page, 30 calls a minute, `market_cap_usd` null for most memecoins, `fdv_usd` present. Jupiter lite-api: no key, a window of 5 calls (bucket reset about 10 s).

## Approach (a default; say if you want it changed)

1. **Two clients**, the shape of `DexScreenerClient`: read only, `timedFetchText` wrapped in `retryingFetchText` (ticket 04), a response cap of 2 MB, only the first 100 entries read, every text field `str()`-cleaned and cut, every number `Number.isFinite`, mints kept only when they match the same address pattern as DexScreener's (`ADDRESS_RE`) before they go into any URL or table.
   - `sources/jupiter.ts` `JupiterTokensClient.topTrending("1h", limit = 50)`: one `GET https://lite-api.jup.ag/tokens/v2/toptrending/1h?limit=50`. Reads per token: mint (`id`), name, symbol, `mcap`, `fdv`, `liquidity`, `holderCount`, `organicScore`, 24 h volume (the buy plus sell volume of `stats24h` if the live shape has them, checked against one real answer and saved as a trimmed fixture), first pool creation time. This module is the only file that names `jup.ag` and only the path prefix `/tokens/v2/toptrending/`.
   - `sources/geckoterminal.ts` `GeckoTerminalClient.trendingPools(page)`: `GET https://api.geckoterminal.com/api/v2/networks/solana/trending_pools?include=base_token&page=N`. Reads per pool: base token mint (from the relationship id `solana_<mint>`), the included token's name and symbol, `fdv_usd` (market cap is `market_cap_usd` when it is present and positive, else `fdv_usd`), `reserve_in_usd` (pool liquidity), `volume_usd.h24`, `pool_created_at`, and the pool's rank.
   - Both take `baseUrl` for tests (`ARGUS_JUPITER_URL`, `ARGUS_GECKOTERMINAL_URL`, mirrored like `ARGUS_DEXSCREENER_URL`; a test points them at a loopback fake server or injects `fetchText`).
2. **One call budget per tick**, `ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_CALLS` (default 5, bounds 1 to 10), counted at the HTTP request, retries included: a counting `FetchText` wrapper sits under `retryingFetchText` and throws a plain `Error("top-down call budget used")` (never retried: it is neither a `TypeError` nor an `HttpError`) on the sixth request. Order: Jupiter first (one request), then GeckoTerminal pages 1, 2, 3. A normal tick makes 4 requests; one retry anywhere still fits. The Jupiter client additionally never makes more than 3 requests in a tick (one plus two retries), so the lite-api's 5-call window can never be reached by this job. DexScreener's budget is separate and unchanged (`ARGUS_META_LEADER_CALLS` 4 `tokenPairs` calls, 30 mints each, for verification).
3. **Merge per token, then filter** (pure, `meta/topdown.ts`, `topDownCandidates({ jupiter, gecko, config })`):
   - **Per-token dedupe:** key by mint; a token seen on both lists, or in several pools, is one candidate. Market cap: Jupiter `mcap`, else GeckoTerminal market cap or `fdv_usd`. Liquidity: Jupiter's token-level `liquidity`, else the sum of that token's pool reserves on the page. Volume 24 h: Jupiter's, else the sum of its pools' `h24`. The candidate keeps its rank on each list and `sources` (`["jupiter"]`, `["geckoterminal"]` or both).
   - **Fake filter** (all three must hold): liquidity at least $50,000 (`ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_MIN_LIQ_USD`), market cap at most 100 times liquidity (`ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_MAX_CAP_LIQ_RATIO`), 24 h volume at least $50,000 (`ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_MIN_VOLUME_USD`). A missing figure fails the test it is needed for (it never passes by being absent). Quote-only tokens (SOL, USDC and the like) and tokens with a generic key (`isGenericKey`) are dropped.
   - **Stock-token exclusion:** a candidate is dropped when `isStockToken({ name, symbol, tags })` says so. Before writing it, read one live Jupiter answer and the TTWO/GTA6 pool answer and record in this ticket's Comments what really marks a tokenized stock (a Jupiter tag, a name suffix such as "xStock" or "Tokenized", the issuer). The rule is then a literal list of markers in code, no pattern built from text, with the TTWO token as a fixture. If nothing reliable marks them, stop and say so (open question 3) instead of guessing.
   - Output sorted by market cap, highest first, ties by mint: `TopDownCandidate { mint, name, symbol, marketCapUsd, liquidityUsd, volume24hUsd, sources, ranks, createdAt? }`.
4. **Wire into the Meta tick.** `RecomputeOptions` gets `topDown?: () => Promise<TopDownResult>` (the worker passes one built from the two clients; a replay passes none). `recomputeMetas` awaits it before the write transaction (no lock held), with a 20 s overall bound; any failure, a timeout or no function means "no top-down candidates this tick" and is logged once (`[meta] top-down sources failed: ...`), never fatal: the previous leaders and the theme lookups still run. `lookUpLeaders` takes its second tier from these candidates (highest source cap first) instead of `trendingCandidates`; the stored `dexscreener:trending` Signals are no longer a leader source (they stay Signals, ADR 0011). Previous leaders stay first (a Meta is never lost to the budget).
5. **DexScreener verifies, with the same filter.** In `lookUpLeaders` a top-down candidate's answer is accepted only when DexScreener's own numbers also pass the three tests (liquidity summed over the token's pairs, 24 h volume summed, market cap taken from the pair with the most liquidity, not the maximum over pairs, so a thin fake pair cannot raise it). A candidate that fails verification is logged (`[meta] leader <symbol> failed verification: <which test>`) and is not a leader. Previous leaders and theme-lookup tokens keep today's rule (max over pairs, no filter), so a Meta already held is not dropped by this ticket.
6. **Bonk launches count as copiers.** `StudyStore` gets `createdBetween(from, to, { includeBonk: true })` (default false, so every other caller is unchanged): the same read plus rows with `pool = 'bonk'`. `LaunchedToken` gains `pool`. `recomputeMetas` groups the union, and a group's counts are kept twice: the existing counts (pump.fun only) feed the launch-theme rows, the base rate, the size floor and the coverage exactly as today; a second set (pump.fun plus bonk) feeds the copies rule (`minCopies`) and the `counts` stored on a Meta or Watching row. The stored launch-theme rows' counts do not change.
7. **Candidates are stored for replay and the War Room.** New table `meta_candidates` (additive, `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` in `migrateMeta`, pruned with the runs): run id, mint, name, symbol, market cap, liquidity, volume, sources, ranks as JSON, `verified` (0, 1 or null), `reason` for a dropped one (`fake: cap 250x liquidity`, `stock token`, `failed verification`). Written inside the run's one transaction. A candidate dropped by the filter is stored with its reason, so "why is the big token not a Meta" has an answer without a replay.
8. **ADR 0002 allowance** (the Operator's decision, question 1, 2026-11-16): in `adr-0002.test.ts` grant `jup.ag` to exactly one module (`packages/core/src/sources/jupiter.ts`), exactly one host (`lite-api.jup.ag`, https, no userinfo, default port) and the path prefix `/tokens/v2/toptrending/`, in the same style as `READ_ONLY_RPC_MODULE` (`export const READ_ONLY_TOKEN_LIST_MODULE` and so on). Everything else the test bans still applies in that file: no `@jup-ag/*` package, no quote or swap host, no key. Add the test's own negative cases (the same module with `quote-api.jup.ag/v6/swap` or a `/tokens/v2/search` path fires).
9. **Config, startup line, pipeline graph, docs.** `ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_CALLS`, `_MIN_LIQ_USD`, `_MAX_CAP_LIQ_RATIO`, `_MIN_VOLUME_USD` in `MetaConfig`/`META_RULE`/`metaConfigFromEnv` (bounded, junk keeps the default), `.env.example`, one `topdown=calls/liq/vol` token on the `[config]` line (`startup-config.ts`), the Meta node in `packages/core/src/pipeline-graph.ts` (two new edges "Jupiter trending" and "GeckoTerminal trending" into the Meta node, a row per knob with `live:`), `pnpm docs:graph`, the hand-written Meta paragraphs of `docs/architecture.md`, and a `--topdown` flag for `scripts/meta-replay.mjs` that runs the two clients once and prints the candidates with their reasons.
