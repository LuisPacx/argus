# 03: "New Meta" replaces Hot now; meme ideas run on a new Meta and every 12 h; summaries and the prompt Meta block follow the new Metas
**Model:** sonnet / high. It decides what argus spends on: the meme trigger, its 12 h refresh and the 5-a-day cap interact, and a wrong order wastes runs. It also deletes Hot now across code, config, pages, graph and docs, and rewrites two prompts that carry untrusted text. The pieces are small; the interplay is the risk.

**What to build:** four changes on top of ticket 01's Metas, decided by the user on 2026-11-15 (`.scratch/cos.md`: "USER all rec: Q22 (a), Q23 (a), Q24 (a), Q25 (a)", Q21 (a) in "META R USER").
1. **New Meta** replaces **Hot now**: a stored event when a subject becomes a Meta, shown on `/meta` and home. Hot now's rank and creator-doubling triggers are removed.
2. **Meme ideas** run when a subject becomes a Meta and again **every 12 h while it stays a Meta**, capped at **8 runs a day** (replaces the Hot now trigger; the Q9 (a) refresh idea is folded in here).
3. **Summaries** (the `meta-summary` task) are written only for the Metas and the Watching subjects (at most 6); "All launch themes" shows counts only.
4. **The judge and propose prompt Meta block** lists the Metas and the Watching subjects (at most 6), each with its leader token and cap, instead of the top 5 launch themes.

## Approach (a default; say if you want it changed)

### 1. New Meta event (replaces Hot now)
- **Trigger (pure, `meta/new-meta.ts`, replaces `hot.ts`):** `newMetaEvents(current, previous)` returns an event for each subject that is a Meta in this run's `meta_current` and was not a Meta in the previous run's. With no previous state (first run, a pause longer than `stateMaxMinutes`, ticket 01) it returns nothing: that run only re-establishes the baseline, so a restart or a gap never raises a burst. Hysteresis (ticket 01) already stops flapping at the entry cap; a subject that fell out and comes back is a new Meta again.
- **Stored in a new table `meta_new_events`** (additive, `migrateMeta`): key, label, raised_at, reason ("became a Meta: $JENSEN at $2.4M, 14 creators in 24 h", written by code), rank, leader mint/name/symbol/cap, copies, run_id; pruned with the runs. Reason for a new table rather than reusing `meta_hot_events`: old rows say "entered top 5" and are about launch themes, and a pre-deploy row must never trigger a meme run. The old table is left unread and unwritten (no migration, no drop); `raiseHotMetas` and its prune line go.
- **Read:** `Datastore.newMetaEvents(since)`. An event is live for `META_RULE.newMetaLiveHours` (6, replaces `hotCooldownHours`); its Meta must still be a Meta in the newest run.
- **Worker:** `metaTick` (`main.ts:337-345`) stores the events after the run, logs `[worker] new meta: "<label>" (leader $X at $Y)`, failure logged and swallowed as `raiseHotMetasSafely` is. Nothing here creates a Narrative, Proposal or Score.
- **War Room:** `GET /api/new-metas` (from `hotNowResponse`, renamed) with the same payload (reason, raised time, the Meta row, matching news from `meta/news.ts`), `NewMeta.vue` (from `HotNow.vue`) and `utils/new-meta.ts` (from `utils/hot.ts`), shown above Current Meta on home and `/meta`; absent while no event is live. Ticket 02's rows read an optional mark from the same lookup (rename `hot` to `newMeta` in `apiRow`). Text only, links only through `safeHttpUrl`.
- **Removed:** `meta/hot.ts`, its tests, `GET /api/hot-metas`, `HotNow.vue`, `utils/hot.ts`, `META_RULE.hot*` and the `hotMetaEvents` store path, the pipeline rows (`pipeline-graph.ts:344-348`). GLOSSARY: delete **Hot now**, add **New Meta**; rewrite **Meme Proposal** (`:31`) to "rides a live Meta" and state its life as 12 h. ADR 0012 addendum gets a note (Hot now is superseded by ADR 0014 and this ticket), ADR 0013 gets an **Amendment 2026-11-15 (meta-redesign)**: the trigger is a new Meta plus a 12 h refresh while it stays a Meta, under the 8-a-day cap; the meme Proposal life is 12 h (was 6 h); nothing else changes (no Spec, no Build). `docs/architecture.md` through `pnpm docs:graph`, plus the hand-written paragraphs (`:90,263,265`).

### 2. Meme ideas on a new Meta and every 12 h
- **Candidates are the current Metas** (`Datastore.currentMetas()`), not events joined to launch rows. A Meta is **due** when it has a live New Meta event it has not been tried for, or when its last started try is at least `MEME_REFRESH_MS` (12 h) old, or it has never been tried. An event bypasses the 12 h gap but not the 6 h trio guard (`hasMemeSince`, `MemeTrioExistsError`), so a Meta that leaves and re-enters inside 6 h is not run twice. Watching subjects get no ideas.
- **Order, so the cap spends well:** New Meta events first (oldest event first), then refreshes by oldest last try. `maxPerPass` stays 3. A failed try is retried after `failedRetryMinutes` (30) as today.
- **Cap:** `DEFAULT_MEME_CONFIG.maxRunsPerDay` becomes **8** (`pass.ts:47`); `ARGUS_MEME_MAX_PER_DAY` may raise it up to the hard ceiling of 40 (today it can only lower), and the live `.env` says 5 until it is changed to 8 at deploy. A meme Proposal stays live **12 h** in `new` (`expiry.ts` `MEME_EXPIRY_MS`, was 6 h), matching the refresh. Cap refusals are recorded once per **slot** (the unique index key `(key, event_at, outcome)`, `meme/store.ts:48`): `eventAt` is the event's `raisedAt` for a new Meta and the refresh slot start (last try plus 12 h) for a refresh.
- **Prompt and snapshot:** `meme/ideas.ts:248,271` stop saying "Hot now"; the prompt names the leader token and its cap ("this Meta is led by $JENSEN at $2.4M; the trigger is: became a Meta / refresh after 12 h") and lists the leader first among the tokens. The stored Proposal reason becomes `New Meta: ...` or `Meta refresh: ...` (`pass.ts:375`), `summaryLine` (`pass.ts:402`) drops the rank for the leader and cap. The snapshot (`MetaSnapshot`, `meme/types.ts`) goes to version 2: `trigger: { kind: "new-meta" | "refresh", reason, at }` and `leader` replace `hot`; version 1 snapshots stay readable (`utils/meme.ts:42,112-113,144` reads both and shows "New Meta: ..." or the old "Hot now: ..."), and missed-ideas/07 (outcome tracking) must read both. Update `.claude/skills/meme-ideas/SKILL.md:3,8` and the `meta-summary` skill text the same way.

### 3. Summaries only for Metas and Watching
- `summariseMetas` (`summary-pass.ts:118`) iterates `currentMetas()` (Metas then Watching, at most 6) instead of the top launch rows. Drop `topRows`, `staleRows` and the stale-rewrite path for lower rows (`:130`); `maxPerPass` becomes 6; the daily cap (40 runs, $0.25) and `minAgeMinutes` (60) stay. Keys are the same group keys, so stored lines, `summaryFresh` and the numbers-line fallback keep working. `metaInputHash` (`summary.ts:129`) adds the leader mint and a coarse cap bucket (a leader going from $1.2M to $3M may rewrite, a 5 % move does not). The prompt (`summary.ts:222`, the `meta-summary` skill) says a Meta is a current trend led by a token with a large market cap, and shows the leader first with its cap.
- "All launch themes" rows get the numbers line only: `metasResponse` (`meta/api.ts`) stops reading stored summaries for the launch-theme list and stops attaching meme ideas to it. Stored old summaries are left in `meta_summaries` and pruned as today.

### 4. Prompt Meta block = Metas + Watching with leader caps
- `selectMetas` (`prompt-block.ts:71`) returns the current Metas then the Watching subjects (at most `maxMetas` = 6, `:28`) from `currentMetas()`, keeping the 120 min age rule and the thin-coverage skip (read the coverage of the run the rows belong to). Lines: `- <label> (Meta): leader <name> ($SYM), market cap $X, N creators copying in 24 h` and `- <label> (Watching, not a Meta): ...`. Same cleaning (`flat`), same `- ` line starts, same fresh-boundary wrapper and 1,200-character cap (check six lines fit; trailing Watching lines are dropped first). `metaRule` (`:133`) is rewritten: current trends led by a token with a large market cap, and subjects being watched that are not Metas; background only, never a score input for the judge, never a name or ticker to reuse for propose. `metaMatchLine` also matches the leader's forms (`rowForms`, `:96`). With no Metas and no Watching there is no block and no rule sentence, as today.

### 5. Config and docs
`.env.example` (`:34-50`: the `ARGUS_MEME_MAX_PER_DAY` default and its value note set to 8; the live `.env` is changed from 5 to 8 at deploy) and `startup-config.ts` (`:64-71`: `meme_runs_24h` shows 8, drop nothing else) updated; pipeline Meta and Meme nodes (`pipeline-graph.ts:330-383`, edges `:425-428`) redrawn: the Meta to War Room edge is "New Meta", the Meta to meme edge is "new Meta, then every 12 h, cap 8 a day"; `pnpm docs:graph`.
