# 01: Launchpad Signals cluster into the Narrative they belong to, not one catch-all
**Model:** sonnet / high — a clustering change on the live Narrative path; the join rule has to be measured on real data (precision first, as in ADR 0007), not guessed. Fixtures and the replay are the proof.

**What to build:** pump.fun create and migrate events (Source `pumpportal:events`, PumpPortal stream) become Signals, and today almost all of them land in two giant Narratives, so a token like Misu never gets a Narrative of its own, never reaches the judge as itself and never becomes a Proposal. Make a launchpad Signal join the thematic Narrative it belongs to (or start its own), and keep any one Narrative from absorbing the stream.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** 03, slice 1 only (the token subject key and clone-cluster grouping, `.scratch/viral-sources/issues/03-meta-ranking-from-launchpad-data.md`). **This ticket depends on 03; it does not feed it.** Why: both need one definition of "the same token name" (same-name copycats here; clone clusters there), and a Meta is defined over tokens, so ticket 03 owns the normaliser and the grouping and this ticket imports them rather than building a second one that could disagree. Ticket 03's ranking needs nothing from the Narrative clusterer (it reads `study_tokens` and launchpad Signals directly), so the dependency runs one way. What this ticket adds to the pair is the Narrative: a Proposal pairs a Narrative with a Tech (GLOSSARY), so a live Meta can only be ridden once a thematic launchpad Narrative exists for it, and 03's judge and propose block reports that match. Also start only after the Phase 3 deploy stack (17, 19, 21, 22) is on `main` and the live DB migrated by it, see Interplay. Independent of ticket 02 (TikTok spike); 02's Source would feed this ticket's cross-source rule once it exists.

**Order (Operator priority, 2026-11-11):** viral-sources 03 Part A, then 04 (Beta finder, top priority), then `warroom-redesign/03` (Current Meta on the home page), then this ticket, then 02. This ticket needs only 03's slice 1, so it can move up if 04 stalls.

Origin: the Operator's "animal going viral on TikTok" idea and the Misu probe, 2026-11-11 (`.scratch/cos.md`, entries "USER IDEA" and "MISU PROBE"). Misu (米苏, a sea lion on TikTok) was created 11-08 04:59 UTC and bonded 11-10 23:52 UTC (about 67 h later). Its TikTok videos were up 1 to 6 days before the create. Argus saw both events as Signals; neither reached a Misu Narrative. Animal names alone do not predict migration (1.8% of animal-named tokens migrate against 2.8% overall, 11-09..11-11, crude regex), so this ticket is about **being able to see a theme at all**, not about predicting winners.

## Why there is a catch-all (checked on a `.backup` copy of the live DB, 2026-11-11, copy deleted)

The code path: `clusterNewSignals` in `packages/core/src/narratives.ts` embeds each unclustered Signal and joins the nearest Narrative in the 72 h window if the cosine similarity to its centroid is at least `JOIN_THRESHOLD` (0.90), else starts a new one. The production embedder (`apps/worker/src/semantic-embedder.ts:133`, ADR 0007) embeds **the title only**.

The launchpad Source writes a fixed-template title (`packages/core/src/sources/pumpportal.ts:305`): `New token on pump.fun: <name> ($<ticker>)` and `Bonded on pump.fun: <name> ($<ticker>)`. What the data shows:

- 58,819 `pumpportal:events` Signals in 5 days (2026-11-06..11-11). 58,491 of them (99.4%) sit in two Narratives: **351** (54,965 creates) and **353** (3,526 bonds, every one of them). Another 324 are in **851**. Three Narratives hold 99.9%.
- **The template is what the model sees.** Embedded with the production model, `New token on pump.fun: <anything>` scores 0.956 to 0.972 against Narrative 351's centroid whatever the name is (`米苏 ($MISU)` 0.968, `Misu ($MISU)` 0.972, `Lil Buddy ($BUDDY)` 0.965). Real news headlines score 0.74 to 0.77 against it. The model compresses all similarities into 0.55 to 1 (ADR 0007), and a six-word constant prefix outweighs a one- or two-word name.
- **The centroid is an attractor.** It is a running mean (`incrementalMean`) over 55k near-identical vectors, so it converges on the template vector itself. Every new create is nearer to it than to any thematic Narrative; "nearest wins" then sends it there. The same holds for 353 and the `Bonded` template (`Bonded on pump.fun: 米苏 ($MISU)` scores 0.959 to 353, 0.911 to 351).
- **It never expires and never stops growing.** Every join refreshes `last_signal_at`, so the 72 h window never closes on 351 (alive since 2026-11-06 18:33, 5 days). There is no cap on Narrative size.
- **A bond carries no name at all.** All 3,526 `Bonded` titles read `Bonded on pump.fun: 9oD9...pump` (the mint abbreviated): the PumpPortal migrate message has no name, so the Signal cannot say what token it is. Only its id (`pumpportal:events:migrate:<mint>`) links it to the create.
- **A thematic Narrative only forms when a non-template Signal seeds it.** 851 ("Big Booty Coin ($BTC)", 324 creates) was started by a title without the prefix, and later same-name creates were nearer to it than to 351. Nothing makes that happen on purpose.
- **Even without the template, similarity alone will not link a social Signal to a create.** Name-only embeddings of Misu variants against "Sea lion Misu TikTok videos go viral in China": `Misu` 0.825, `Misu ($MISU)` 0.810, `米苏 ($MISU)` 0.841, `Sea Lion Misu` 0.896, all under 0.90. Two Misu creates against each other score 0.961. So the cross-source link needs a rule on the shared name or ticker; a lower global threshold would over-merge news (ADR 0007: over-merges start at 0.89 on the tuning sample).
- **The catch-all is also judged and ranked first.** `narrativesToJudge` (`datastore.ts`, ~line 1087) orders by `signal_count DESC`, minimum 2 Signals (`DEFAULT_JUDGE_MIN_SIGNALS`). 351 and 353 have been judged 8 times each (total 2.2 to 3.2, "routine pump.fun launch notices"), and sort ahead of every news Narrative whenever they change materially. They have no Proposals.
- The DB holds no social Signal about Misu (the Sources are RSS, Google Trends, GitHub trending, DexScreener, PumpPortal), so the Misu replay can only prove the launchpad half. 9 Misu/米苏 creates (11-08 04:59 to 11-10 23:26, spelled `米苏` or `Misu`, ticker `MISU` or `Misu`) and 1 bond (`9oD9...pump`, 11-10 23:52) are in 351 and 353 today.

## Design constraints (the approach is for the implementer to choose and measure)

Candidate mechanisms, none mandatory; the acceptance below is what counts. A combination is expected.

1. **Embed the subject, not the template, for launchpad Signals.** The embedded text is the token name and ticker only (the part the creator wrote), never `New token on pump.fun:`. This needs the Signal to carry its subject (a new optional Signal field persisted by an additive, idempotent column, or the prefix stripped by one function used by both the embedder and the tests). The recorded embedder id of the Narratives it builds changes with it (ADR 0007: vectors of different ids are never compared), so old Narratives, 351 and 353 included, stop being joined and age out of the 72 h window.
2. **A bond joins the Narrative of its create, by mint.** The Signal ids carry the mint on both sides; when the create is known (a Signal row, or `study_tokens.name` for the 11-09+ window), the bond takes its create's subject. A bond with no known create keeps today's behaviour (own template Narrative) or stands alone; it must not feed a catch-all.
3. **Same-name copycats are one Narrative.** Creates that ticket 03's `subjectKey` / `groupTokens` put in one group (name or ticker equal after case-folding, punctuation and `$` stripped, CJK kept, generic keys excluded) join one Narrative. Copycat count is itself the momentum evidence for the judge (Misu had six or more copycats). Use 03's functions; do not write a second normaliser (it is a linear matcher, never a RegExp built from the name: worker rule, names are creator-written and untrusted).
4. **A cross-source rule on a distinctive shared token.** A non-launchpad Signal (news, trends, DexScreener, a later TikTok Source) joins a launchpad Narrative when it shares a distinctive name or ticker token with it. "Distinctive" means rare among the recent Signals (a document-frequency cut, reusing 03's generic-key stoplist), so `coin`, `ai`, `trump`, `big` do not link. Measure it on a labelled set the way ADR 0007 did; precision before recall.
5. **A size bound.** No Narrative holds more than a configured number of Signals (suggested default 2,000, `ClusterConfig.maxNarrativeSignals`); once full it is not joinable and the next matching Signal starts a successor. Also stop `last_signal_at` alone from keeping a flooded Narrative alive indefinitely if that proves a problem in the replay.
6. **Singletons.** There are about 12k creates a day and most are one-off junk; 2,715 of the 3,306 Narratives are already single-Signal. Do not make this worse: a lone create may still start a Narrative as today (the judge ignores fewer than 2 Signals), but the replay must report the Narratives and judge-eligible Narratives per day so the Operator sees the cost. If the War Room list gets visibly noisier, say so in Comments rather than redesigning it here.

Out of scope: re-clustering Signals already stored (ADR 0007: existing Narratives are left alone and age out), changing the `JOIN_THRESHOLD` for news, changing the Rubric or the judge prompt, changing what the Study or `study_tokens` record, and any trade, wallet or chain call (ADR 0002 is untouched: this ticket reads Signals the Source already stores and adds no host, so ticket hardening/06 is unaffected).

## Acceptance

Tests are offline in `pnpm check` (fake `Embedder` through the existing seam, as in `narratives.test.ts` and `narrative-calibration.test.ts`); the real-model replay is an opt-in script like `pnpm verify:semantic` and is not part of `pnpm check`.

- [ ] **Misu fixture forms one Narrative.** A committed fixture of the real Misu Signals (the 9 creates and the bond, titles and ids as stored, plus 20 other creates with unrelated names) clusters so that the 9 Misu creates and the bond are in one Narrative that holds nothing else, and the 20 others are not in it (tested, failing first on `main`).
- [ ] **A related social Signal joins it.** Add a synthetic non-launchpad Signal that names the subject ("Sea lion Misu TikTok videos go viral in China") and one that does not ("Federal judge halts building work on Trump's triumphal arch"): the first joins the Misu Narrative, the second does not (tested). The rule behind it is stated in a comment with the measured numbers.
- [ ] **A common word does not link.** Creates named `Big Coin`, `AI Coin`, `Trump Coin` and a news headline about "big AI coin and Trump" end in different Narratives or alone, not merged through the shared common words (tested with the document-frequency cut in the fixture).
- [ ] **A bond follows its create.** A bond whose create is in the fixture joins that create's Narrative, whatever the name's script; a bond with no known create does not join any thematic Narrative and does not seed an unbounded one (tested).
- [ ] **No Narrative absorbs the stream.** A test floods N+1 same-name creates (N = `maxNarrativeSignals`): no Narrative holds more than N Signals and the extra starts a successor (tested). Its default is documented where `JOIN_THRESHOLD` is.
- [ ] **No template attractor.** A test with 1,000 distinct random-name creates from the real title template shows no Narrative holding more than 5% of them (the shipped code puts all of them in one).
- [ ] **Replay on a DB copy, recorded in Comments.** Copy the live DB with `sqlite3 .data/argus.db ".backup <home-disk path>"` (never open the live file with argus code; delete the copy afterwards), load its 58k `pumpportal:events` Signals with their original published times into a throwaway datastore, and run the new clustering with the real model (opt-in script, model from `~/.cache/argus/models`). Report in Comments: (a) the Misu creates and bond land in one Narrative that contains no other token's Signals; (b) the largest Narrative and the share of launchpad Signals in the top 3 (today 99.9%; target: the top 3 hold under 10% and the largest is at most `maxNarrativeSignals`); (c) the number of Narratives with 2 or more launchpad Signals per day, since those are the new judge-eligible ones; (d) the 60 largest launchpad Narratives with their titles, read by hand, with a note on any that are not one subject.
- [ ] **News is unchanged.** `narrative-calibration.test.ts` and the 320-headline and 146-headline measurements give exactly today's numbers (53 of 112 same-story pairs joined, 0 cross-story over-merges at 0.90); `scripts/measure-narrative-threshold.mjs --embedder fixture` before and after in Comments.
- [ ] **Docs.** ADR 0007 gets a dated amendment (launchpad Signals are embedded by subject, the Narrative size bound, the recorded embedder id change), or a new ADR if the implementer judges it a new decision; GLOSSARY needs no new term unless the implementer finds one missing (then note it for `/domain-modeling`). The tests' fixtures never put untrusted creator text in a RegExp.
- [ ] Full `pnpm check` passes once before the commit.
