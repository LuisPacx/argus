# 07: Idea outcome tracking and the weekly review hit rate
**Model:** sonnet / medium. Plain code that reads stored rows at fixed offsets and one addition to the weekly review; no new Brain task.

**What to build:** argus now suggests meme ideas (ticket 06). Without a measurement it cannot tell whether they are good. For every `meme` Proposal, record the Meta's stats at the time it was made, its outcome at +6 h and +24 h, and the later tokens that match the idea's name or ticker with their own outcome. The weekly review reports the **hit rate** against the base rate (1.3% of creates migrate after 60 s, from viral-sources/04's backtest, `.scratch/viral-sources/issues/04-beta-finder.md`).

**Status:** open

**Blocked by:** 06 (the `meme` rows and `meta_snapshot` this ticket reads). Agreed order: warroom-redesign/10 slice c, then 03, 04, 05, 06, **07**.

Origin: `.scratch/missed-ideas/issues/01-nvidia-ceo-miss-gap-and-fixes.md`, answer Q12 (2026-11-14).

## Decisions already made (do not reopen)

- **Stats at creation:** the Meta's row as stored in `meta_snapshot` (tokens, creators, migrations unfiltered and after 60 s, top market cap, rank); no re-reading later rows for it.
- **Outcome at +6 h and +24 h** of the Meta: migrations after 60 s (the measure that is not bundle-driven; unfiltered is stored too but labelled) and top market cap, read from the Meta runs and `study_tokens` at or before that time.
- **Later matching launches:** tokens created after the idea whose normalised name or ticker matches the idea's (the same normaliser as the distinct-name rule, `meta/key.ts`), each with its own outcome (migrated, migrated after 60 s, peak market cap when stored). Matching is linear, no dynamic RegExp.
- **Weekly review:** the hit rate = ideas with at least one matching later token that migrated after 60 s, over ideas, against the base rate of 1.3%, with a Wilson 95% interval and the count of independent Metas (several ideas for one Meta are not independent). With small numbers it says so plainly and states no lift.
- Idea quality is **not** rated by the Operator in this ticket; Shortlist and Dismiss reasons are shown next to the numbers, not used as labels.

## What to build

- A store table or columns for the measurements (additive, idempotent migration, rehearsed on a `.backup` copy deleted afterwards), written by a worker tick that fills the +6 h and +24 h outcome once the time has passed and re-checks later matches for a bounded period (suggested 7 days, config).
- No look-ahead: an outcome at +H reads only rows at or before creation + H (tested, as in the backtest).
- The weekly review output (the existing review, `docs/agents` or the review code, find by "weekly review") gains the hit-rate section and a per-idea table with the Meta stats, outcomes and matches.
- War Room: the Proposal page of a `meme` shows its measurements (text only); `/meta` shows nothing new.

## Acceptance

Offline in `pnpm check`.

- [ ] **Measurements on fixtures.** A fixture idea with a Meta snapshot, a later matching token that migrated after 60 s and one that did not yields the expected +6 h and +24 h outcomes and matches; an idea with no matches reads 0 hits (tested).
- [ ] **No look-ahead.** A row created after creation + H that would change the outcome does not (tested).
- [ ] **Idempotent and bounded.** A second tick writes nothing new; matching stops after the configured window; a worker restart in between loses nothing (tested).
- [ ] **Hit rate.** The review computes the hit rate, the base rate 1.3%, the Wilson interval and the independent-Meta count on a fixture; with 2 ideas it reports "too few to say" and no lift (tested, wording checked).
- [ ] **Name matching.** A later token whose name differs only by case, `$`, punctuation or emoji matches; one sharing only a word does not; a 10,000-character name and RegExp metacharacters run in linear time (tested).
- [ ] **War Room shows it.** The `meme` Proposal page shows the stats at creation, the outcomes and the matches as text; markup in a token name is not rendered as markup (render test). **Browser check** in the Argus Chrome profile (`~/.local/share/argus/chrome`, never the user's own), phone width and 1240 px; screenshot path in Comments.
- [ ] **Docs.** `docs/architecture.md` and the `/pipeline` graph (`pnpm docs:graph`) show the measurement step; the ADR from ticket 05 gets a dated note that the idea is measured.
- [ ] Full `pnpm check` passes once before the commit.
