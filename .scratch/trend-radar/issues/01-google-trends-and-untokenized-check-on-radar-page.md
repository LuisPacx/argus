# 01: Google Trends rising terms and the untokenized check on a War Room Radar page
**Model:** sonnet / high. This ticket sets the trust boundary every later radar ticket reuses: third-party terms are stored, rendered and sent to DexScreener as search text. The care is the one cleaner, the fail-closed check and the rule that a read never writes.

**What to build:** the smallest slice that is useful alone. The worker polls Google Trends' realtime RSS for the US every 15 minutes, keeps each term with its traffic, drops anything that is a current Meta or Watching subject, runs the **untokenized check** on every new term (DexScreener search through the existing client, the ticket 16 live-token test imported from `meme/live-token.ts`, and the local pump.fun creates), and a new War Room page `/radar` lists the terms with their coin-side state. No model, no X, no second country, no buttons. Useful alone: the Operator opens one page and sees what is rising on Google right now that has no live coin, instead of reading Google Trends and DexScreener by hand.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** none
Spec: `.scratch/trend-radar/spec.md` (sections 3, 4.1, 5.1, 5.4, 5.5), ADR 0016. The moment scanner's tickets are not needed for this one.

## Evidence and design (read before building)

- Reuse, do not copy: `packages/core/src/sources/google-trends.ts` (the feed URL, `parseRssItems`, `tag`, `decodeEntities` from `sources/rss.ts`, `timedFetchText` and `BackoffSource`-style backoff from `sources/backoff.ts` and `sources/http.ts`); `DexScreenerClient.search` (`sources/dexscreener.ts`); `isLiveToken` (`meme/live-token.ts`); the current Metas and Watching subjects the Meta pipeline stores (`meta/store.ts`, `meta/leaders.ts`); the PumpPortal create Signals (`pumpportal:events:create:<mint>` rows in `signals`, see `Datastore` near line 1684) and `study_tokens` rows (including `source` `chain_migrated` and `chain_sample` from Phase 3 ticket 20) for local creates.
- The radar is **not a `Source`**: it polls through its own reader (`packages/core/src/radar/readers/google-trends.ts`) and writes no row to `signals`, so it adds no entry to `source-class.ts` and changes no Narrative. The existing `trends:google` Source keeps polling the US on its own; do not touch it. One extra request every 15 minutes is the whole duplicate.
- Switch `ARGUS_RADAR=1`, off by default, like `ARGUS_MOMENTS`. Junk values are an error, as for the other settings. A dry run polls nothing.
- Countries are data: `radar/config.ts` has `googleGeos: ["US"]` here; ticket 02 widens it. Caps, thresholds and the 14-day seen-set length live in the same file, constants only, the environment may lower and never raise.

## Build

1. `packages/core/src/radar/key.ts`: **no new normaliser.** `radarKey(term)` is a thin wrapper that imports `normaliseKey` and `aliasKey` from `packages/core/src/meta/key.ts` (what "the same name" means for Metas) and returns `aliasKey(normaliseKey(term))` ("GTA VI", "GTA 6", "GTA6", "#GTA6" give `gta6`). Nothing else in the radar normalises text. If the moment scanner's `spellings.ts` (its ticket 05) exists, it too is built on `meta/key.ts`; import its spelling variants for topics, not for keys.
2. `packages/core/src/radar/clean.ts`: `cleanTerm(text)`: flatten, cut to 300 characters, strip markup (the existing untrusted-text helper in `sources/untrusted-text.ts`); `searchWords(text)`: letters, digits and spaces only, 1 to 6 words, each cut to 40 characters, empty means "refuse". These are the only two ways third-party text reaches storage and a search.
3. `radar/readers/google-trends.ts`: for each geo in config, GET `trends.google.com/trending/rss?geo=XX`, return candidates `{ sourceId: "radar:google:XX", key, term, trafficText, trafficMin, newsTitle, newsUrl (safeUrl only), at }`. `trafficMin` is the number in "2000+". Status per source: `ok`, `backing off` (network error, 429, 5xx, honours Retry-After, doubling wait up to 1 hour), `format changed` (a non-empty body that parses to zero items, or a body that is not the feed), `down`. An empty answer is never "no trends".
4. Store (`radar/store.ts`, tables created idempotently in the worker's database, read-only view for the War Room as the moment store does): `radar_readings` (source, key, term, traffic, news title and link, seen at; append only), `radar_seen` (key, first_seen, last_seen), `radar_trends` (one row per key: label, first_seen, last_seen, score, shown, coin-side state with its time, trace JSON, local-creates count and creators, last checked), `radar_source_state` (source, status, reason, last ok, since). Keys are kept 14 days.
5. `radar/check.ts` (the untokenized check, spec 5.5): `traceTrend(trend, { dex, localCreates, now })`. Search each cleaned phrase (the key's words here); keep Solana pairs whose name or ticker matches; create time at or after `first_seen` minus 48 hours, **but a pair with no `pairCreatedAt` fails closed toward taken-side: it is kept, because dropping it could read a live coin as absent and call the Trend untokenized**; run each through `isLiveToken` (import it); lookalikes keep their reason; coin-side state `taken` (a live token at $50,000 or more of market cap), `contested`, otherwise `open`; local creates from one in-memory index of normalised names of the last 48 hours of stored creates, built once per tick (no RegExp from any text). **Precedence of the final state, first match wins:** `not traced` (a search failed), `taken`, `contested`, `tokenizing` (5 or more distinct local creators, no live token), `untokenized` (coin-side `open` and 4 or fewer creators). A live token always outranks the local count; `tokenizing` outranks `untokenized`. A search that throws is `not traced` and keeps the last good trace.
6. `radar/skip.ts`: a term whose `radarKey` equals the `radarKey` of the label or of a merged theme label of a current Meta or Watching subject is dropped before it is stored as a Trend (kept in `radar_readings` only). **Both sides are folded by the same function**, so a Meta labelled "GTA 6" skips the Google term "gta vi" and the reverse.
7. `radar/job.ts` and a worker step in `apps/worker/src/main.ts`, re-entrant like the other jobs: every 15 minutes poll, store, keep the seen-set, skip, check. **Shown rule for this ticket:** a key new in the seen-set whose `trafficMin` is 1,000 or more. At most 30 DexScreener searches a tick, jittered, a new Trend first, then re-checks of live Trends every 60 minutes (live: 24 hours after last seen). Ticket 02 replaces the shown rule with the score.
7a. **Source alert.** A source in `backing off`, `format changed` or `down` for more than 1 hour (Google Trends included) shows a banner on `/radar` and `/` with the source, the state and the time since its last good poll, through the same banner the page uses for the other source states; it clears itself on the next good poll. Before the Google feed is first polled for real, the agent checks the feed's terms and `trends.google.com/robots.txt` for the `/trending/rss` path as ticket 02 does for each news feed, and writes the result in Comments (the existing `trends:google` Source already reads the same URL; a disallow or a terms bar means stop and tell the Operator).
8. War Room: `GET /api/radar` (read only; **at most 100 Trends and 50 lookalikes per Trend in one answer**, newest score first, so a bad day cannot make an unbounded page; the source states and last good poll) and `/radar` (`apps/warroom/app/pages/radar.vue`), text only, one row per Trend: term, traffic, age, coin-side state with the biggest live token and the lookalikes with reasons, the local-creates line ("12 creates in 48 h by 9 creators; local creates are a partial view"), the news title as text with its link only through `safeHttpUrl`. Folded below the list: `taken` and `tokenizing`. A status block per source. A link in the War Room's navigation, and a one-line strip on `/` ("N Trends shown, M untokenized") that links in.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Key** (`key.test.ts`): `radarKey` is `aliasKey(normaliseKey(x))` from `meta/key.ts` (a test fails if `radar/` defines its own normaliser or numeral table); "GTA VI", "gta 6", "GTA6", "#GTA6" give one key; "Super Bowl LX" is not rewritten; a term of only punctuation or emoji gives no key; 10,000 characters are cut; no RegExp is built from the input (the repo lint rule passes).
- [ ] **Cleaner** (`clean.test.ts`): hostile text (`<img src=x onerror=1>`, `from:elonmusk`, `"a" OR (b)`, control characters, newlines, 100,000 characters) is stored flattened and cut with markup removed, and `searchWords` returns letters, digits and spaces only, 1 to 6 words, or a refusal.
- [ ] **Reader** (`readers/google-trends.test.ts`, recorded feed XML, fake fetch): a recorded feed gives its 10 items with traffic ("1000+" is 1,000) and news links; a 429 with Retry-After is `backing off` and honours the wait; a 200 with a non-feed body is `format changed`; a feed with zero items from a non-empty body is `format changed`; none of these is stored as "no trends" or touches the seen-set; a `javascript:` news link is dropped.
- [ ] **Seen-set** (`store.test.ts`, real Datastore on a temp dir via `testing/temp.ts`): a key seen again updates `last_seen` and makes no second Trend; a key not seen for 14 days and seen again is new; an old database without the tables opens and gets them; readings are append only; the War Room's view cannot insert (type test).
- [ ] **Skip rule** (`skip.test.ts`, real `currentMetas` output from a fixture): a term equal to a current Meta's label, to one of its merged theme labels, or to a Watching subject is not stored as a Trend; a different term is; **a Meta labelled "GTA 6" skips the Google term "gta vi", and a Meta labelled "GTA VI" skips "gta 6" and "GTA6"** (both sides folded by `radarKey`).
- [ ] **Untokenized check** (`check.test.ts`, fake DexScreener and a fixture of local creates): a drained pool (the Boomazoom case: $131K cap, pool of $4) is a lookalike with its reason and the Trend stays `untokenized`; a live token at $60K is `taken`; live tokens under $50K are `contested`; 5 distinct local creators is `tokenizing` and 4 is not; the live test is the imported `isLiveToken` (a test fails if `check.ts` redefines the $5,000 or 100x numbers); a search that throws is `not traced`, keeps the last good trace and is never `untokenized`; a pair created before `first_seen` minus 48 hours is ignored, **but a live pair with no `pairCreatedAt` is kept and makes the Trend `taken` or `contested`, never `untokenized`**; precedence on fixtures: a live $60K token plus 6 local creators is `taken`; live $20K plus 6 creators is `contested`; no live token plus 5 creators is `tokenizing`; no live token plus 4 is `untokenized`; a failed search plus anything is `not traced`; the local index matches creates by normalised name without building a RegExp.
- [ ] **Job** (`job.test.ts`, fake reader, fake DexScreener, fake clock): a poll stores readings and creates a shown Trend only above the traffic bar; 30 searches a tick are never exceeded; live Trends are re-checked hourly and a dead one (24 hours unseen) is not; a source in `backing off` skips its turn and says so; `ARGUS_RADAR` unset or `0` does nothing; a dry run polls and searches nothing.
- [ ] **Source alert** (`radar-alert.test.ts`): a source `backing off` for 61 minutes shows the banner with its last good poll time; at 59 minutes it does not; a good poll clears it; a Google feed that returns `format changed` shows it.
- [ ] **Row cap** (`radar.test.ts`): with 500 stored Trends and 80 lookalikes on one, GET returns 100 Trends and 50 lookalikes, highest score first.
- [ ] **API and render** (`apps/warroom/server/test/radar.test.ts`, `radar-render.test.ts`): GET returns only the listed fields; a hostile term renders as one clamped line of escaped text; a non-http(s) link is never an anchor; the empty and "source down" states show; a GET leaves every table unchanged (compared before and after); the War Room process writes no radar table; no `v-html`.
- [ ] **Browser, seeded** (`pnpm page:check --seed /radar`; add a `{trend}` seed with an untokenized Trend, a `taken` one, a `not traced` one, a hostile term and each source status; say in the box that the run was seeded; never the user's Chrome): exit 0, no console, page or request errors, no horizontal overflow at 390 or 1240 px, screenshots read.
- [ ] Type-check (`tsc --noEmit --checkers 1`), `pnpm lint` and the full `pnpm check` pass, run once before the commit.

## Constraints

- Follow `docs/agents/worker-rules.md` and `CODING_STANDARDS.md`. No RegExp built from a term, a headline or a token name. The worker never fetches a URL found in a feed. The radar makes no Signals and no `source-class.ts` entry (ADR 0016).
- Do not copy `isLiveToken`, the $5,000 floor or the 100x ratio: import them.
- Commit subject `Trend radar ticket 01: Google Trends rising terms and the untokenized check on a Radar page`, body ends with `Ticket: .scratch/trend-radar/issues/01-google-trends-and-untokenized-check-on-radar-page.md` before the trailers. No `Closes #N`.
