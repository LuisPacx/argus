# 04: A news Narrative that fails the buildability gate can become a `meme` Proposal (the news story as the subject)
**Model:** sonnet / high (a new Proposal source for the `meme` kind, its subject, cap and legitimacy rules, a unique-index change and an ADR amendment).

**What to build:** today a news Narrative with no buildable Tech goes nowhere: the buildability gate drops it (`narrativesToPropose`, `datastore.ts:1625`), and the Nvidia CEO story of 2026-11-12 was lost that way (ADR 0013, first paragraph). The user approved Q9 (b) on 2026-11-15: a news Narrative that **fails the buildability gate** can become a **`meme` Proposal**, written by the meme prompt (`meme-ideas`, `meme/ideas.ts`) with **the news story as the subject** instead of a Meta. The Operator then Shortlists or Dismisses it like any meme idea (no Spec, no Build, no Score of its own). This **amends ADR 0013**, which says a meme comes only from a Meta, and the proposal-routing ticket 01 amendment, which says memecoin ideas come only from Metas.

## What is there today (re-read 2026-11-15, after meta-redesign/03)

- **The gate and the selectors.** `narrativesToJudge` (`datastore.ts:1552`) takes news Narratives with a market Signal (ADR 0011); `narrativesToPropose` (`datastore.ts:1625`) takes the scored ones whose latest buildability is at least the Rubric's `gateMinScore`, with `n.kind = 'news'`, `HAS_MARKET_SIGNAL` and `NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM proposals p WHERE p.narrative_id = n.id)` (`:1637`, any kind of Proposal). The propose pass re-checks kind and gate at creation (`propose.ts:547`). A news Narrative below the gate has a Score and no Proposal, forever.
- **The `meme` kind is built around a Meta.** `createMemeProposals` (`datastore.ts:991`) needs a Meta key and a snapshot (`meta_key`, `meta_snapshot`, immutable by trigger, `datastore.ts:483-495`); a `meme` row has `narrative_id` NULL (`:478`); one live trio per key per 6 h window (`proposals_meme_live`, `:490`, `MEME_WINDOW_MS`); the snapshot is `MetaSnapshotV1 | MetaSnapshotV2` (`meme/types.ts:48`, `:54`, `:82`). A meme Proposal lives 12 h in `new` (`MEME_EXPIRY_MS`, `expiry.ts:16`).
- **The pass.** `generateMemeIdeas` (`meme/pass.ts:262`) works a queue from `dueMetas` (`:234`): a New Meta event, or a refresh every 12 h (`MEME_REFRESH_MS`); Watching subjects are allowed in the queue. The trio guard is `hasMemeSince(meta.key, now - 6 h)` (`pass.ts:348`, `datastore.ts:1063`). Names must not copy a token of the Meta's group or one DexScreener lists (distinct-name rule: `parseMemeOutput` in `meme/ideas.ts:84`, `copiedNames` `:150`, `dexCopies` `meme/pass.ts:478`). The prompt is `memeIdeasPromptFor` (`meme/ideas.ts:233`), with the Meta's matching news (`meta/news.ts`, `matchingNews` `:45`) in a second untrusted block.
- **Caps.** The meme pass has its own daily cap of **8 runs** (`DEFAULT_MEME_CONFIG.maxRunsPerDay`, `pass.ts:52`; hard ceiling 40, `MAX_MEME_RUNS_PER_DAY`, `:38`), checked through `memeIdeasSpendSince` (`:296`), and the research budget (`researchUsage`, `:302`) shared with judge, propose and summaries. Separately, `researchProposalsSince` (`datastore.ts:1052`) counts Proposals with `narrative_id IS NOT NULL` for the "30 a day" research cap.
- **The unique index.** `proposals_narrative_id` (`datastore.ts:463`) is `UNIQUE (narrative_id) WHERE narrative_id IS NOT NULL`, and `createProposal` throws `NarrativeAlreadyProposedError` on any row with the id (`:933`).
- **Legitimacy.** The Rubric's legitimacy dimension (v3: impersonation, fraud, and hate or harassment are not generated; satire about a public figure is fine and labelled) is read from the stored Rubric into the meme prompt.
- **War Room.** `memeView` (`apps/warroom/app/utils/meme.ts:109`) reads `metaSnapshot` (version 1 and version 2) and builds the numbers line and the trigger line.

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-15)

1. **Q1 Duplicates with Metas:** skip when the story matches a current Meta's subject (headline match via `meta/news.ts`); the Meta's trio wins. A Watching subject also counts as a subject to match.
2. **Q2 Lifecycle:** 12 h life (the existing meme expiry); one trio per Narrative ever.
3. **Q3 Tech path independent:** a news-meme Proposal does not block a later Tech Proposal; `narrativesToPropose` excludes only Tech (`kind = 'narrative'`) Proposals.
4. **Q4 Link and cap:** the meme Proposal sets `narrative_id`; the 30-a-day research-cap count excludes `kind = 'meme'`.
5. **Q5 Naming:** same `meme` pill, plus a "from news: <headline>" line, a link to the Narrative and the failing Score.
6. **Q6 Market Signal:** keep the rule (ADR 0011: only Narratives with a market Signal are judged). Unscored big news is its own ticket if there are no news memes in a week.
7. **Q7 Floors:** legitimacy >= 5, hook >= 6, momentum >= 6, and buildability below the gate.
8. **Q8 Cap sharing:** shares the 8-a-day meme cap; Metas are served first.
9. **Q9 Trigger:** the latest Score counts, the newest Signal is under 12 h old, and it fires when a re-score lifts the Narrative over the floors.
10. **Storage:** `meta_key = narrative:<id>` plus a snapshot variant (the sketch default).

## Approach

1. **Selector.** A new `Datastore` read, `narrativesToMeme(embedder, since, gateMin, floors, limit)`: `kind = 'news'`, `HAS_MARKET_SIGNAL`, latest Score with buildability **below** `gateMin` and legitimacy >= 5, hook >= 6, momentum >= 6 (Q7), `last_signal_at` within the last 12 h (Q9), no `kind = 'meme'` Proposal with `meta_key = 'narrative:<id>'` ever (Q2). Never a launchpad Narrative. Read only, tested in `propose-store.test.ts`.
2. **Skip Metas' subjects (Q1).** In the pass, before spending a run, drop a candidate whose title matches a current Meta or Watching subject by `headlineNamesKey` / `matchingNews` (`meta/news.ts:30`, `:45`) against the current Metas' keys. No RegExp built from a headline.
3. **Subject.** The meme prompt gets the Narrative (title, summary, newest Signals as untrusted text in a fresh boundary, the Score's reasons) where it gets a Meta today; same output (3 ideas, same fields, same validator `parseMemeOutput`), same distinct-name rule against DexScreener results (`dexCopies`; no Meta group to compare to).
4. **Storage.** `meta_key = narrative:<id>`, `meta_label` the headline, `narrative_id` set and `score_id` the failing Score. A snapshot variant `{ version: 3, kind: "narrative", narrative, score, news }` beside the Meta ones (`MetaSnapshot`, `meme/types.ts:48`). The 6 h `meme_window` and `proposals_meme_live` work unchanged; "one trio ever" is a check on any `kind = 'meme'` row for the key (not `hasMemeSince`'s 6 h window).
5. **Index and counts.** Change `proposals_narrative_id` to `UNIQUE (narrative_id) WHERE narrative_id IS NOT NULL AND kind = 'narrative'` (drop and recreate, idempotent, in the same migration block, `datastore.ts:463`), so a trio can share the id and a Tech Proposal can follow (Q3). `NarrativeAlreadyProposedError` (`:933`) and `narrativesToPropose` (`:1637`) look only at `kind = 'narrative'` rows. `researchProposalsSince` (`:1053`) adds `AND kind <> 'meme'` (Q4). The Narrative list's `has_proposal` and `hasProposal` (`:1480`, `:1500`) keep meaning a Tech Proposal; check that no other reader of `narrative_id` breaks.
6. **Pass.** One more source of work in `generateMemeIdeas` after the Metas (Q8: Metas first), under the same 8-a-day cap and the same `meme-ideas` task: no new Brain task, no new host. A re-check at creation time that the Narrative is still eligible (as in `propose.ts:547`).
7. **War Room.** `memeView` reads the new snapshot variant next to the two it reads today; the card shows "from news: <headline>" (rendered as text), links the Narrative, and shows the failing Score.
8. **Docs.** ADR 0013 amendment (a meme comes from a Meta, or from a news Narrative that fails the gate and passes the floors), the proposal-routing/01 amendment text, GLOSSARY (**Meme Proposal**, **Narrative**), `docs/architecture.md` and the pipeline graph (`pnpm docs:graph`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `narrativesToMeme` returns a `kind = 'news'` Narrative with a market Signal, a latest Score with buildability below the gate, legitimacy >= 5, hook >= 6 and momentum >= 6, and a Signal under 12 h old; it returns none of: a launchpad Narrative, one below any floor, one with an older newest Signal, or one that already has a `meme` trio (one trio ever). A re-score that lifts a Narrative over the floors makes it appear. Tests in `propose-store.test.ts`.
- [x] A qualifying Narrative has at least one news Signal, meaning a Signal whose Source is outside `MARKET_SOURCE_IDS`. A Narrative made only of `dexscreener:trending` or pumpportal Signals (token names; DexScreener token Signals are still embedded into news Narratives, `narratives.ts:216`, and legacy rows 3881 and 4059 are of this kind) never takes the news meme path. Test in `propose-store.test.ts`: a Narrative with only market-Source Signals is not returned, and the same Narrative with one headline Signal added is.
- [x] Q1: the pass skips (no run, nothing stored) a Narrative whose story matches a current Meta's or Watching subject; the Meta's trio is made as before.
- [x] Q3: `narrativesToPropose` and `createProposal` ignore `kind = 'meme'` rows: a Narrative with a news-meme trio is still returned by `narrativesToPropose` once its buildability reaches the gate, and gets a Tech Proposal; a Tech Proposal still blocks a second Tech Proposal. `proposals_narrative_id` is unique over `kind = 'narrative'` only, and the migration is idempotent on an existing database.
- [x] Q4: `researchProposalsSince` counts no `kind = 'meme'` row; a test with a trio of news memes and one Tech Proposal counts 1.
- [x] Q8: with the 8-a-day meme cap short (for example 1 run left), a due Meta is served before a news Narrative; a news Narrative is stored only while the cap and the research budget have room, and a capped candidate is recorded and retried later, as Metas are.
- [x] The pass creates one trio of `meme` Proposals for an eligible Narrative through the fake Brain: the news as the subject in a fresh untrusted boundary, `narrative_id` set, `meta_key = narrative:<id>`, the snapshot variant holding the Score and news, a name-copy retry once then fail closed, and a 12 h life (`expiry.ts`); nothing is stored when the Narrative stops being eligible at creation time.
- [x] The card and the Meme page show the same `meme` pill, a "from news: <headline>" line, a link to the Narrative and the failing Score; hostile text in a headline or summary is rendered as text and cannot end the prompt boundary. Version 1 and 2 snapshots still render as before.
- [x] ADR 0013 amended, the proposal-routing/01 amendment text pointed to it, GLOSSARY and `docs/architecture.md` agree; `/pipeline` shows the new edge from the Narrative or judge node to the meme node (`pnpm docs:graph`).
- [x] `pnpm page:check` on a meme card of each source at 390 and 1240 px (no horizontal scroll at 390); full `pnpm check` once before the commit.
