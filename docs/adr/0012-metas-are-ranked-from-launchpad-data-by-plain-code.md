# Metas are grouped and ranked from launchpad data by plain code

Status: accepted (viral sources ticket 03, 2026-11-11; the rank order was decided by the Operator the same day).

A Meta is what the token market is launching around right now (GLOSSARY: Meta). Argus finds the live ones itself, from the pump.fun creates and migrations the Study already stores (`study_tokens`), every 15 minutes, for the last 6 h and 24 h. The grouping, the counting and the ranking are plain code in `packages/core/src/meta/`. The Brain is not used for grouping or ranking. Its only part is one optional line: the top five Metas of the 24 h ranking each get a one-line summary (`meta-summary`, haiku-class, at most 40 runs and $0.25 a day, reused until the input moves and 60 minutes pass), and a templated numbers line stands in whenever there is none.

A Meta is not a Narrative and not a Proposal. A Narrative is argus's own cluster of Signals, a Proposal pairs a Narrative with a Tech, and a Meta is observed on the market. The ranking is context: it is shown on the War Room home page and `/meta`, reaches the judge and propose prompts as background, and creates no Narrative, Proposal or Score, and changes no Rubric dimension.

Decisions:

- **Clone clusters first.** Tokens that share a normalised name or ticker key are one group (union-find; `meta/key.ts`, `meta/group.ts`). A stoplist and a hub guard keep generic keys (`coin`, `ai`, `btc`) from fusing unrelated groups. Names are creator-written, so every matcher is linear and none is built from them.
- **Rank order: distinct creators, then migrations more than 60 s after the create, then market cap, then token count** (`META_RULE.rankOrder`, overridable with `ARGUS_META_RANK_ORDER`). The reason: raw token count is the cheapest figure to inflate (one ticker alone was about 1,400 tokens from 11 creators in the three-day copy), and distinct creators count people instead of launches. On the replay for 2026-11-11 20:00 UTC the order puts the ni hao Meta first and GTA VI third. Same-name clones whose migrations are all bundled with the create (DeepSeek, IShowSpeed) rank below organic Metas; that is intended.
- **Migrations are unfiltered.** `migrated_at` is PumpPortal's receipt time and the bot filter (Phase 3 ticket 04) is not built, so every figure says "(unfiltered)", and `migrationsAfter60s` is stored beside it as the crude organic proxy. The run's note says the 6 h window under-counts slow migrators.
- **Theme Metas by shared subject word, not by embedding** (`meta/theme.ts`). A clone cluster of at least 30 distinct creators is an anchor. Its subject forms are the whole name or ticker keys, spaces removed, that at least 10% of its tokens carry (ni hao: `nihao`, `mihao`; GTA VI: `gta6`, `gtavi`). Any other cluster joins an anchor when at least half its tokens have a word, or two adjacent words joined (`ni hao` is `nihao`, `gta 6` is `gta6`), equal to a form of that anchor, of at least 4 characters and not generic. A cluster is judged by its share of tokens, so one bridge token (`Ni Hao Ansem` inside the Ansem cluster) does not pull a cluster in; a cluster matching two anchors joins neither; anchors never merge with each other and small clusters never merge with each other. `ARGUS_META_THEMES=0` turns it off. The group keeps the anchor's key and label.
- **The theme rule was chosen by measurement, precision first.** The labelled set is the launchpad creates of 2026-11-10 20:00Z to 2026-11-11 20:00Z from a `.backup` copy (`packages/core/src/fixtures/meta-theme-labelled.json`): 977 labelled tokens in 131 clone clusters, namely 76 ni hao clusters (257 tokens), 33 GTA VI clusters (130 tokens) and 22 clone clusters that must stay separate (590 tokens, including ELYSION, Bernie, FORK, BAG, DeepSeek, IShowSpeed, `el gato`, `arigato`, `higher`, Trump, Elon). The labels are hand-made and the criteria are in the fixture; the Ansem cluster is left unlabelled (the ni hao cat's profile picture is Ansem, so it is related) and a test only checks that its one `Ni Hao Ansem` token does not pull it in. Result: zero wrong merges among labelled pairs. The ni hao spellings go from 76 groups to 11, with 244 of the 257 tokens in the largest group (146 before); GTA VI goes from 33 groups to 21, with 91 of 130 in the largest (78 before). On the whole replay window the ni hao group grows from 144 tokens and 82 creators to 260 and 156, GTA VI from 78 and 56 to 91 and 65. Looser rules (any shared word, no anchor size) were tried on the same data and made visible wrong merges (36 `Nigga Nuke` tokens into `Hope`, `LP TEK` into `FIRST PROPER RINGS PAD`, `NEED FOR SPEED` into `IShowSpeed`), which is why the anchor, the share and the length floor exist.
- **Name embeddings were measured and rejected.** The production gte-small (ADR 0007), titles only, cannot separate the members that share no word from the clusters that must stay apart: `米好` and `咪好` score 0.811 against `ni hao cat`, the same as `Bugles Cat` (0.816) and `HI ANSEM` (0.822) and below `Wang Hao Inu` (0.855) and `Knee Hao` (0.866); `woohao` scores 0.839, between them. `GATO` scores 0.761 against `GTA VI`, below unrelated names such as `Claudia` (0.776). No threshold keeps zero wrong merges and merges those, so none is used.
- **What the rule does not do.** Spellings that share no word with the anchor (`咪好`, a pun on ni hao; a bare `GATO`; `WASTED`) are not merged; those tokens were not in the three-day copy either, so the named examples could not be tested literally. Unlabelled merges on two other days were read by hand and mostly look right (`Fomo X` into `Fomo`, `GTA 6 X` into GTA6); a few are uncertain (`Nipples Very Distracting Alice` into the Alice Huang spy cluster). The theme rule is a gain in recall for the two biggest Metas, not a model of themes.
- **The prompt block** (`meta/prompt-block.ts`). The judge and propose prompts carry the newest top five Metas of the 24 h ranking (label, distinct tokens, creators, migrations unfiltered, market cap when known) as untrusted data inside the prompt's fresh random boundary, reusing ticket 24's block shape (ADR 0011): labels are cleaned, control and invisible characters stripped, whitespace flattened, each line starts with `- `, the block holds at most 1,200 characters (ticket 24's context block keeps its 1,500) and the context block still closes the prompt. When a launchpad Signal of the Narrative names a token that carries a form of a top Meta, one line says which Meta and its rank. A ranking run older than two hours is not shown. No Metas, no block and no rule sentence: the prompt is exactly what it was without them. The Rubric is unchanged and the block is not a score input.

This contradicts nothing in ADR 0002 (the job reads Study rows and the public DexScreener API that other Sources already use; no wallet, no signing, no chain call), ADR 0006 or ADR 0007 (Narrative clustering is unchanged; the embedder is not used for Metas).
