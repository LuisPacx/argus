# The trend radar: a Trend is its own thing, a Trend idea is a `meme` Proposal with a trend source, and stage B pays for X's official API under a spend cap

Status: accepted (the Operator chose Option B, built in two stages, on 2026-11-19; `.scratch/trend-radar/spec.md`). The caps and thresholds named here are proposals he may change; the decisions are not. Stage A (free sources) is built first; the X API in stage B is not turned on until the Operator has read stage A's 14-day scorecard and written "go B" (ticket 09).

Argus has no finder for a subject that is new, rising and not yet tokenized. News Narratives need a market Signal to be judged (ADR 0011), a Meta needs a token at $1,000,000 (ADR 0014), and the moment scanner finds clips inside a topic someone already named (ADR 0009 amendment). The trend radar finds the subject. Three choices need a record.

## Decisions

1. **A Trend is not a Source, a Signal or a Narrative.** The radar reads Google Trends' rising terms, a list of news feeds of its own and, in stage B, X's trend counts into its own tables. It makes no row in `signals`, joins no cluster and never starts a `judge` run or a Tech Proposal. It has no entry in the Source class table (ADR 0011 does not apply, as it does not for a Moment). Its feed list is `config/radar-feeds.json`, apart from `config/rss-feeds.json`: adding feeds to the latter would add Signals and change Narrative clustering, whose margin is thin. A subject that is a current Meta or a Watching subject is not a Trend (it is tokenized by definition). The "untokenized" state reuses the ticket 16 live-token test (`meme/live-token.ts`) by import: one definition of a live token.

2. **A Trend idea is a `meme` Proposal with a trend source, pressed by the Operator. Not a new kind, and not automatic.** The Operator presses "Make ideas" on a Trend; the War Room writes one request row; the worker runs the `meme-ideas` path with the Trend as a fenced, untrusted block and files three ordinary `meme` Proposals (12-hour life, Shortlist or Dismiss, no Spec, no Build, no score). The marker is snapshot version 5 (`kind: "trend"`, `meta_key = trend:<id>`, trigger `trend-operator`), the evidence links are the Trend's source links and DexScreener pair links, and names to avoid are the traced clones and lookalikes. It counts in the angle allowance (10 in any rolling 24 hours) and the research budget, not in the automatic 8-a-day cap; one request for the same Trend in 24 hours. This is the fifth source of a `meme` Proposal after a Meta, a news story, an Operator angle and a Moment, for the reasons ADR 0013's Moment amendment gave: every behaviour the idea needs already hangs off `kind = 'meme'`, and a source marker is cheaper to change than a kind. Automatic ideas from a Trend wait, as the scanner's do, until the Operator has read 14 days of scorecard.

3. **Stage B may spend real money on X's official API, behind a cap in code.** Pay-per-use, two endpoints only (Trends, $0.010 a request; Counts recent, $0.005 a request), app-only read token, one declared host in one module. A daily cap of $3.00 (rolling 24 hours; about $2.44 is the planned use) and $90 over 30 days, both in code, lowerable and never raisable by configuration (architecture invariant 3); a request that would pass either is not sent; a request of unknown cost is charged at its price; an unreadable ledger counts as spent. Off by default (`ARGUS_RADAR_X_API=1`), dry run supported. The key is read from the git-ignored `.env`, never printed, never passed to a child. The Operator prepays small amounts; the prepaid balance is a hard stop beyond the code.

4. **Hand-off to the moment scanner goes through its topics table and its cap of 4 active topics.** By hand in stage A ("Watch on X", an Operator topic). Automatically in stage B (off by default): topics of source `trend`, at most 2 of the 4 slots, never displacing a Meta or an Operator topic, 12-hour life, at most 6 a day. The radar builds no scanner of its own and adds no X page loads.

5. **Plain code counts and thresholds; the model only names and filters.** One haiku task, `radar-name`, no tools, no API fallback, a batch every 30 minutes, 48 runs a day (ceiling 96 in stage B), taken from the research budget. The model never extracts terms from headlines; it names what counting found.

## Considered options

- **A Trend feeds Proposals automatically (a new kind, or automatic `meme` ideas).** Rejected for now. Its input is third-party text from sources whose precision is unmeasured; automatic runs on noise spend the research budget and the 12-hour life fills the War Room. Revisit after the 14-day scorecard, the way the moment scanner's ticket 11 does.
- **A Trend as a new Narrative kind (`radar`) in clustering.** Rejected. It would put feed terms into the clusterer (thin margin, ADR 0007), the judge path would need a market Signal that an untokenized subject by definition lacks, and it would blur the line ADR 0011 drew.
- **Add the feeds to `config/rss-feeds.json` and read Signals.** Rejected for the reason in decision 1; it also makes the radar depend on the Narrative pipeline's timing.
- **Option C, topic-free X scanning in the browser.** Not chosen. About 750 page loads a day on the one shared account (3.7 times what ADR 0009 sized, about 60 times the loads ever tried), and a lock stops the Study and the scanner. It needs its own decision and a second ADR 0009 amendment, and stays off until the 14 days show a gap only it could fill.
- **Third-party X data APIs.** Rejected as ADR 0009 rejected them: they rely on scraping X, can vanish or reprice, and carry the same terms exposure.
- **X Search with engagement filters.** Not used: $0.005 a post returned, about $360 a month for one polled query.
- **No X API, stage A only.** A real option and the default until the Operator says "go B". The bar for going (spec section 8): stage A is worth keeping, and at least half of the good subjects it missed were first seen on X.

## Is paying for the X API a good choice? (recommendation)

Yes, in stage B, small and staged, not before stage A's data. Why it is reasonable: about $75 a month fits inside the $100 of real cash the architecture names (stage A costs $0); it adds no browser load to the shared account, so it adds no ban risk to the Study or the scanner (the one risk that cannot be undone); the browser route for the same signal costs more in risk than the API costs in dollars; and X's official API is the only route X's terms allow, which ADR 0009 kept as "the compliant path". Why it is gated: the research did not read a spending limit on the pricing page, billing of re-read posts is unstated, and the value is unproven until stage A shows its misses are X-shaped. The cost of being wrong is bounded: a week of prepaid use is under $20, and the code cap and the prepaid balance both stop it.

## Consequences

- New: `packages/core/src/radar/` (readers, key, score, check, config), `config/radar-feeds.json`, a Brain task `radar-name` and skill, tables owned by the worker (the War Room reads them through its read-only view and writes only request and mark rows), a War Room page `/radar`, and in stage B one module that names the X host. GLOSSARY gains Trend, Untokenized and Radar.
- ADR 0013 gains a pointer to decision 2. ADR 0009 is not amended: the radar reaches X's browser only through the moment scanner's topics, under that scanner's caps. ADR 0011 and ADR 0002 are not amended: the radar is not a Source, reads no chain, holds no wallet, launches nothing, and `adr-0002.test.ts` bans swap and RPC hosts, not the X API host; stage B adds its own tripwire test pinning the one module that names it.
- The architecture's "No X as a Source" line stands: X trend counts are used by the radar, not as Signals.
- Invariant 1 (keys) names the new key; the dev server's allowed `.env` names gain one exact name by an Operator-approved change (ticket 10).
- Untrusted input: every term, headline, trend name and token name is third-party text and gets the existing treatment (cut, flattened, fenced with a fresh boundary, no tools, rendered as text, no RegExp from it, no URL fetched). Text that reaches a search goes through one cleaner that can never add an operator.
- argus still never posts, likes, follows, messages or launches (ADR 0002, invariant 6).
