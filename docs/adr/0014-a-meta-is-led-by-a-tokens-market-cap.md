# A Meta is led by a token's market cap

Status: **Superseded in part by 0018 (2026-11-22):** the $1,000,000 enter and $500,000 stay leader market-cap rule, the copies rule and Watching by cap are replaced by graduations on one subject; market cap is now display only. Still valid: the cap of 3 Metas, hysteresis, staleness and the state carry, `meta_current`, New Meta, summaries, the prompt block, the meme trigger and plain code deciding Metas. History below is kept as written. Originally: accepted (Meta redesign ticket 01, 2026-11-15; decided by the Operator). Supersedes the ranking part of ADR 0012 (the rank order and the list of what a Meta is); see "What stays" below. **Discovery superseded by ADR 0015 (2026-11-16):** how a leader and its subject are found (the "Subject and leader" bullet, the "trending Signal is a candidate" bullet, the second step of the lookup order, and open question 2) is now top-down from Jupiter and GeckoTerminal trending, with an AI grouping step; the thresholds, Watching, hysteresis, budget rules and storage below still stand.

A Meta is a current trend with a token really high: millions of market cap, lasting minutes to days, at most 3 at a time. The NVIDIA CEO case is the model: one subject, many copies, one token far above them. Argus finds it from the token's market cap, not from how many creators copy a theme. Plain code, no model call; the Brain has no part in deciding what is a Meta.

Why the old definition failed. ADR 0012 ranked launch themes by distinct creators and used market cap only as a late tie-break for the top ten groups, and `/meta` asked for 50 rows. The result was about 50 "Metas", many with one token, none of them a trend with a high token. Run 143 (24 h window, 50 rows): none at or above $250k, the best a Jensen group at $93k. At 15:35Z the Jensen token (`Jensen Huang ($JENSEN)`) stood at $96k, created a day before the Jensen group's window, so no group saw it. Over the stored history 24 of 143 runs had a row at or above $1M, so "No Meta right now" will be the normal state most of the time. That is intended.

Decisions:

- **Subject and leader.** A subject is a launch theme group (ADR 0012: clone clusters and theme merging; key from `meta/key.ts`). Its leader is the token of any age with the highest current market cap that carries the subject, found two ways: Solana tokens in the `dexscreener:trending` Signals, and the biggest token of each top launch theme (the lookup the ranking already makes). A token matches a subject when one of its subject-key forms equals the group's key or the whole name or ticker of one of the group's tokens, never through a generic key (`coin`, `ai`) or one shorter than 4 characters; no pattern is built from a name.
- **Copies.** The subject needs at least 10 distinct creators in the last 24 h (`ARGUS_META_MIN_COPIES`) to become a Meta or to be Watching. A leader with no group, or a group below 10, is neither. The copies rule applies to becoming; staying depends on the leader's market cap alone (Operator decision, 2026-11-15). Effect: a Meta can outlive its launch wave while the token holds.
- **Becomes a Meta** when the leader's market cap, looked up this run, is at least $1,000,000 (`ARGUS_META_ENTER_MCAP_USD`) and the copies rule holds. **Stays a Meta** while the leader is at least $500,000 (`ARGUS_META_STAY_MCAP_USD`); between the two it is `holding` (shown as cooling by ticket 02). The gap is hysteresis: a token hovering at $1M does not flicker in and out. At most 3 Metas (`ARGUS_META_MAX_METAS`), highest leader cap first, ties by key. A stay value above the enter value keeps both defaults.
- **Watching** (`ARGUS_META_WATCHING`, 3): the top 3 other subjects by leader cap that are below $1M and meet the copies rule. Not a Meta, labelled so everywhere. A Meta that falls below $500k, or is crowded out by a fourth over $1M, is no longer a Meta; below $1M it competes for Watching like any subject. There is no Watching floor (the Operator may add one later).
- **Caps are looked up every Meta tick, within a budget.** `ARGUS_META_LEADER_CALLS` (4) DexScreener `tokenPairs` calls per tick, 30 addresses each, beside the existing launch-theme lookups. Order: the previous Metas' and Watching leaders first (a Meta is never lost to the budget), then trending candidates by the figure stored on their Signal, highest first, then the rest. A lookup that fails or falls outside the budget leaves that subject's status and last cap as they were, marked stale; it never drops a Meta and never creates one. The carry ends when the figure was last read from DexScreener more than `ARGUS_META_STATE_MAX_MINUTES` ago (`freshAt` on the leader, kept from run to run): re-saving a carried row in a new run does not renew it, so a lookup that keeps failing, or `ARGUS_META_LEADER_CALLS=0`, cannot hold a Meta for ever. Three failed calls in a row end the tick's lookups. State older than 120 minutes (`ARGUS_META_STATE_MAX_MINUTES`, a pause) is not carried.
- **The trending Signal is a candidate, not a price.** A Signal is stored once (`INSERT OR IGNORE`), so its figure is the one seen first. `dexscreener:trending` Signals now say `market cap $N` (and `FDV $N` when different) in their summary; the Meta tick reads them back (last 24 h by `ingested_at`) only to name candidates and order the lookups. The current cap always comes from the lookup. A token that stays high for days stays a candidate only through the carry-over of the previous tick's leaders; a token the feed first showed days ago that was never a leader is not seen unless a launch theme names it (open, below).
- **Stored in one new table.** `meta_current` (run, kind `meta` or `watching`, rank, key, label, leader, holding, entered_at, counts and top tokens as JSON), additive, pruned with the runs. The subject's `key` is the group key, so summaries, matching news and meme Proposals keep working unchanged. `counts` are the group's own 6 h and 24 h counts (zero when no copy was launched in 24 h), so a Meta never depends on its subject being inside the 50 stored launch-theme rows.

What stays from ADR 0012: grouping, theme merging, the size floor, coverage, the launch-theme rows (`meta_rows`, `/api/metas`, now "All launch themes", counts only), summaries, Hot now, the prompt block and the matching news. They keep running unchanged on the launch-theme ranking until tickets 02 (pages) and 03 (event, meme trigger, summaries, prompt) move them onto Metas; each is marked in ADR 0012 with the ticket that changes it. What this ADR supersedes: ADR 0012's rank order as the definition of a Meta, and its "what a Meta is" list.

A Meta is still not a Narrative and not a Proposal; this creates no Narrative, Proposal or Score and changes no Rubric dimension. No wallet, signing or chain call is added (ADR 0002): the job reads Study rows and the public DexScreener API through the declared client.

Update (meta-redesign ticket 03, 2026-11-15): the pieces left on the launch-theme ranking above now run on Metas. **New Meta** replaces Hot now: one event (`meta_new_events`) each time a subject is a Meta in this run and was not in the run before (nothing on a first run, after a restart, or after a pause longer than `ARGUS_META_STATE_MAX_MINUTES`); it is shown on home and `/meta` for 6 hours while the subject is still a Meta, and it starts the meme ideas run (ADR 0013, amended). Summaries are written only for the Metas and the Watching subjects (at most 6 in all); launch themes show counts only. The judge and propose prompt block lists the Metas then the Watching subjects (at most 6), each with its leader token and cap, labelled "Watching, not a Meta" for the second kind.

Open: (1) a Watching floor (default none; a $2k token can be Watching when nothing else exists); (2) polling the trending feed live in the tick instead of using stored Signals plus the carry-over (one more call a tick; default no). Decided: staying needs no copies, only the $500k cap.

Update (ADR 0015, 2026-11-16): open question (2) is answered yes and discovery moves to a live top-down poll (Jupiter `toptrending/1h` and GeckoTerminal `trending_pools`, DexScreener only to verify), with an AI step that groups the trending tokens and the top 30 themes into named subjects and a rule fallback. The copies rule now counts pump.fun and bonk launches. Everything else in this ADR stands.
