# A Proposal has a kind: `narrative` or `meme`

Status: accepted (missed ideas ticket 05, 2026-11-15; decided by the Operator on 2026-11-14, missed-ideas/01 answers Q5, Q9, Q10, Q11, Q12 and Q14).

Until now every Proposal pairs a Narrative with a Tech (GLOSSARY: Proposal), is scored against the Rubric with buildability as a hard gate, gets a Spec when shortlisted and is built. That path cannot surface a joke token about a live story: the Nvidia CEO case was lost because the Narrative was scored down for legitimacy and there is no Tech worth building for a meme. A Proposal now has a **kind**: `narrative` (today's) or `meme`.

A `meme` Proposal is an idea for a token that rides a live Meta (GLOSSARY: Meme Proposal). It is generated from a Meta that is Hot now (missed-ideas/03; see the 2026-11-15 amendment at the end: now a new Meta and every 12 h), not from a Narrative (one exception: a news Narrative that fails the buildability gate, see the news meme amendment at the end), and has no Tech.

Decisions:

- **`proposals.kind`** is a column with the values `narrative` and `meme`. Every existing Proposal reads `narrative`. The column, its idempotent migration, the task, the cap and the UI are built by missed-ideas/06; this ADR is the contract it builds against.
- **A `meme` Proposal pairs a Meta with an idea.** It stores the Meta's label and an immutable snapshot of the Meta as it was at generation (counts, top tokens, matching news), so the card stays readable after the Meta moves. It has no Narrative and no Tech. A Meta stays context (ADR 0012): the Proposal cites a Meta, it does not turn the Meta into a Narrative or a Score.
- **Fields (Q14):** name, ticker, angle (the joke and why now), how it differs from the leader (name copies are rejected; the distinct-name rule of `packages/core/src/beta/` is the check), a short description, an X first-post hook, an image idea as text, risk labels (for example "real person") and evidence links (the Meta's top tokens and the matching news). No image or logo is generated.
- **Generation (Q10; amended 2026-11-15, below):** only on the Hot now trigger, at most once per Meta per 6 h, one sonnet run that produces 3 ideas, with its own cap of 40 runs a day taken from the research budget (the judge plus propose share, architecture section 5), not added to it. The room-left ranking from viral-sources/04 slices 1 and 2 is an input to the prompt.
- **Legitimacy comes from Rubric v3 (missed-ideas/04) and nowhere else.** Memes and satire about public figures are allowed; impersonation, fraud, and hate or harassment are not. The risk labels are how a `meme` Proposal says "real person" without scoring itself; the prompt reuses the v3 wording, so the Rubric stays the single source and the meme task keeps no second copy of the rule.
- **Lifecycle (Q9):** Shortlist, Dismiss with a reason, and Why. **No Spec, no Build and no Rubric score of its own**; the buildability gate does not apply, because there is nothing to build. A `meme` Proposal in `new` expires after **6 h** (counted from creation, as `new` already is) unless it is shortlisted; a shortlisted one follows the existing 7 days. It shows as a list filter on Proposals and under each Meta on `/meta`. Telegram notification is not part of this decision.
- **Outcome tracking (Q12, missed-ideas/07):** the Meta's stats at creation, the outcome at +6 h and +24 h (migrations after 60 s, top market cap), later launches that match the name or ticker and how they did, and a weekly review of the hit rate against the 1.3% base rate. The kind is measured from the start.

What it replaces:

- **The planned `beta` kind** (viral-sources/04 slices 3 and 4: the Beta proposal task and its War Room). They are superseded and never built. The backtest of viral-sources/04 had already shown no lift (the switch `ARGUS_BETA_PROPOSALS` stayed off); the 2026-11-18 re-run still runs and its result feeds missed-ideas/06. The room-left ranking and the distinct-name rule (slices 1 and 2) stay, as inputs. The GLOSSARY term Beta stays as the name of the launch idea; a Beta is proposed as a `meme` Proposal, and no "Beta Proposal" entry is added.
- **The Operator's decision of 2026-11-11** that a `meme` kind waits (Phase 4) until one tag lesson has replicated on a later Cohort. Q5 (2026-11-14) supersedes it: meme ideas start now, with no Spec and no Build, and are measured from the first one instead of gated on a lesson.

What it contradicts:

- **The GLOSSARY definition of Proposal** ("a Narrative paired with a Tech and a token angle"), which is amended to cover the second kind, and the "Proposal status" expiry rule (48 hours in `new`), which gains the 6 h `meme` exception. Anything that assumed every Proposal has a Narrative, a Tech, a Score and a Spec must read `kind` first.

What it leaves alone:

- **ADR 0002:** argus never launches, signs or spends. A `meme` idea is text for the Operator to act on outside the system; the image idea is text and no image is made.
- **ADR 0011:** eligibility of a Narrative is unchanged. A `meme` Proposal is not eligibility-gated by a market Signal of a Narrative; its market evidence is the Meta's own launchpad data, which is market class. News stays context and never raises the trigger.
- **ADR 0012:** a Meta is ranked by plain code and stays context. The Hot now event (its addendum) is the trigger, and the event itself still makes no Narrative, Proposal or Score: the meme task, run after it, makes the Proposal (ADR 0012's "no Proposal" describes the ranking and the event, not what is built on them); the summary path and the prompt blocks are unchanged.
- ADR 0003 (one build archetype) is untouched: a `meme` Proposal never reaches the Build.

## Amendment 2026-11-15: Tech Proposals come only from news Narratives (proposal-routing ticket 01)

This narrows the first half of "what a Proposal is made from" and sharpens the second.

- Memecoins never get a Tech. A Tech is a small web app, and one token's creates give it nothing to ride.
- A `narrative` Proposal (a Tech Proposal) comes only from a Narrative of kind `news`, still with a market Signal (ADR 0011) and the buildability gate. The judge selects only `news` Narratives, the propose pass selects only `news` Narratives, and the creation re-check drops an answer, counted as withdrawn, when the Narrative is not `news` at that moment.
- A `launchpad` Narrative (one token's creates from the pump.fun stream) is routing and context only: it is still clustered, listed (War Room Narratives page, Kind = Launchpad) and used by `contextNarratives`, and is never judged or proposed from. Its judge minimum (`ARGUS_JUDGE_MIN_SIGNALS_LAUNCHPAD`) is removed. A launchpad Narrative that already has a Score keeps it as history. The Activity feed lists news Narratives only.
- Memecoin ideas come from Metas (this ADR's `meme` kind), and from one other place: a news Narrative that fails the buildability gate (see the 2026-11-15 news meme amendment at the end). Never from a launchpad Narrative.
- Proposals 154 to 157 (made from launchpad Narratives before this rule) are left to expire as usual; no data is rewritten.

## Amendment 2026-11-15 (meta-redesign ticket 03; decided by the Operator)

Hot now is gone (ADR 0012's addendum is marked superseded; ADR 0014 defines a Meta by a token's market cap). The "Generation" decision above changes, nothing else does (no Spec, no Build, no Score, the same fields, the same legitimacy rule):

- **Trigger.** Plain code, no news: a subject becomes a Meta (a New Meta event, `meta_new_events`), and again **every 12 h while it stays a Meta**. A Meta is due when a live event is newer than its last started try, or when its last started try is 12 h old, or when it was never tried (a Meta already there at deploy); a failed try is retried after 30 minutes. An event bypasses the 12 h gap but not the 6 h trio guard (one trio per Meta per 6 h, whatever became of the last), so a Meta that leaves and comes back inside 6 h is not run twice. Watching subjects get no ideas.
- **Order and cap.** New Metas first (oldest event first), then refreshes by oldest last try, at most 3 Metas a pass. The daily cap is **8 runs** in any rolling 24 hours (was 40, then 5 by configuration), taken from the research budget as before; `ARGUS_MEME_MAX_PER_DAY` may now raise it up to the hard ceiling of 40. A cap refusal is recorded once per slot (the New Meta event, or the refresh slot start: the last try plus 12 h) and the pass logs what waited.
- **Life.** A `meme` Proposal in `new` lives **12 h** (was 6 h), matching the refresh, so a Meta that stays a Meta always has live ideas. A shortlisted one keeps the 7 days.
- **Snapshot version 2.** A new Proposal stores the Meta (its subject, entry time, counts, top tokens), its leader token and cap, and the trigger (`new-meta` or `refresh`, with the time) instead of a launch-theme row's rank and the Hot now event. Version 1 snapshots of Proposals made by Hot now stay readable and are not rewritten; readers (the War Room, and outcome tracking in missed-ideas/07) read both versions. The stored reason reads "New Meta: ..." or "Meta refresh: ...".

## Amendment 2026-11-15 (proposal-routing ticket 04; decided by the Operator): a news story can be the subject of a meme

The opening paragraph of this ADR says a `meme` Proposal comes from a Meta. That has one more source. A news Narrative with no buildable Tech was dropped by the buildability gate and went nowhere (the Nvidia CEO story of 2026-11-12 was lost that way). Now **a meme comes from a Meta, or from a news Narrative that fails the buildability gate and passes the floors**. The Operator then Shortlists or Dismisses it like any meme idea: no Spec, no Build, no Rubric score of its own. This amends the proposal-routing/01 amendment above ("memecoin ideas come only from Metas") and nothing else in it: a launchpad Narrative is still routing and context only, and a Narrative that passes the gate is still proposed as a Tech exactly as before.

- **Which Narrative.** Kind `news`, with a market Signal (ADR 0011 stands: only a Narrative with one is judged, so only one has a Score) and at least one news Signal (a Source that is not `market`; a Narrative made only of token-name Signals from `dexscreener:trending` or PumpPortal is not a story). Its latest Score has buildability **below** the gate and legitimacy at least 5, hook at least 6 and momentum at least 6 (`NEWS_MEME_FLOORS`), and its newest Signal is under 12 h old. A re-score that lifts it over the floors makes it eligible; the latest Score is the one that counts. `Datastore.narrativesToMeme` is the one selector.
- **Metas first, and the Meta wins a shared subject.** A story whose headline names a current Meta or a Watching subject (the headline match of `meta/news.ts`) gets no trio of its own: the Meta's trio covers it. The pass serves Metas first, then stories, under the same meme cap (8 runs a day, `ARGUS_MEME_MAX_PER_DAY`), the same research budget and the same `meme-ideas` task (no new Brain task, no new host). A capped story is recorded and tried later, as a Meta is.
- **One trio per Narrative, ever.** A story is never run twice, whatever became of its trio. Stored as `meta_key = narrative:<id>` with the usual 6 h window, plus `narrative_id` and `score_id` (the Score it was made on). A trio lives 12 h in `new` like any meme.
- **The Tech path is independent.** A trio does not block a later Tech Proposal for the same Narrative: `proposals_narrative_id` is unique over `kind = 'narrative'` rows only (idempotent migration: the old index is dropped and made again), and `narrativesToPropose`, `createResearchProposal` and the Narrative list's `hasProposal` look at Tech rows only. A trio is not counted in the 30-a-day research cap (`researchProposalsSince` leaves `meme` rows out), only in the 8-a-day meme cap.
- **Snapshot version 3.** The snapshot is `{ version: 3, kind: "narrative", narrative, score, trigger, news }`: the story, the Score it failed the gate with, what the code decided and the news Signals as they were. Versions 1 and 2 stay readable. At creation time the Narrative is judged again on the same terms (the selector, for that one id); one that stopped qualifying while the model ran stores nothing.
- **Same safety.** The story is third-party text: headline, summary, news and token-name Signals and the judge's reasons go into one block that ends only at a boundary line of fresh random bytes, flattened and cut, and no RegExp is built from any of it. The same parser, the same distinct-name rule against DexScreener (one retry, then nothing stored) and the same legitimacy text from the stored Rubric apply. argus still launches nothing (ADR 0002).

## Amendment 2026-11-17 (meta-redesign ticket 10; decided by the Operator): a meme idea lives 12 hours the worker is running

The 12 h life of a `meme` Proposal in `new`, and the 12 h meme refresh, count time the worker was running. The worker writes a heartbeat (`worker_alive`) each tick and at boot; a silence over `WORKER_GAP_MS` (10 min) is recorded as a gap in `worker_gaps`. A meme idea expires at `created_at + 12 h` plus the gaps after its creation that began before it ran out (no cap), and the refresh is due at the last try plus 12 h plus the same. Narrative Proposals (48 h, 7 days) and a shortlisted meme (7 days) stay wall-clock. Nothing already stored is rewritten.

## Amendment 2026-11-17 (meta-redesign ticket 11; decided by the Operator): a meme can also come from an Operator's angle on a current Meta

A meme can also come from an angle the Operator types on `/meta` for a current Meta or a Watching subject, run by the worker under the same caps. The War Room writes one `meme_angle_requests` row (insert only; the worker claims and resolves it) and never a Proposal. The worker runs the same `meme-ideas` path, with the angle as a third fenced block of the prompt, named untrusted, under its own fresh boundary (the instruction that the ideas are about it sits outside the fence; the output, risk-label and name rules are not the Operator's to change), and files a trio of `meme` Proposals that carry `operator_angle` (`{ requestId, angle, sourceUrl, urlDropped }`) and a version 2 snapshot whose trigger is `operator-angle`. The first http(s) link of the text is split out by code (stored only if it is a plain address; the prompt gets its host, never the address, and nothing fetches it). An angle trio is keyed by its request (`meme_window` is the request id, negated), so it is not blocked by the Meta's automatic trio and does not block it; the automatic 6 h guard is unchanged. An angle run counts in the research budget and in an allowance of its own (ticket 12: 5 angle runs in a rolling 24 h, ceiling 10, marked by `brain_runs.angle`; `dailyMemeRunLimit` is the one place that says how), not in the 8-a-day meme cap, and automatic runs cannot use it, at most 5 requests in a rolling 24 h (`ARGUS_MEME_ANGLE_MAX_PER_DAY` can only lower it; the code ceiling is 10), one waiting at a time, and the same cleaned angle for the same Meta is refused for 24 h. A request ends `done`, `refused`, `failed` or `capped` (never left waiting by a cap, including the 30-a-day new Proposals cap); a rate limit, a shutdown or a dry run leaves it waiting. The Proposal is an ordinary `meme` Proposal: 12 h life, shortlist or dismiss. argus still launches nothing (ADR 0002).

## Amendment 2026-11-19 (moment scanner; the Operator approved amending this ADR for it on 2026-11-19): a meme can also come from a Moment, as a `meme` Proposal with a moment source, not a new kind

A Moment (GLOSSARY; `.scratch/moment-scanner/spec.md`) is a concrete thing spreading on X inside a Watched topic, found by the scanner under ADR 0009's 2026-11-19 amendment. It is not a Meta (no token leads it), not a news Narrative (it is not a Signal and ADR 0011 does not apply) and not an Operator angle (the Operator did not type it). The question this amendment settles: is an idea made from a Moment a new `kind`, or a `meme` Proposal with a fourth source?

**Decision: a `meme` Proposal with a moment source. No new kind.** `proposals.kind` stays `narrative` or `meme`.

Why:

- **Everything the idea needs already hangs off `kind = 'meme'`.** The fields (name, ticker, angle, difference, description, hook, image idea, risk labels, evidence links), the 12-hour life and its downtime rule, the 7-day life once shortlisted, Shortlist and Dismiss with a reason, the absence of a Spec, a Build and a Rubric score, the list filter and the meme cards, the name-copy check against DexScreener, the legitimacy text from the Rubric, the daily and research caps, and outcome tracking all key on `meme`. A new kind would have to be added to each of those switches for no difference in behaviour.
- **The repo already extends `meme` by source, not by kind.** The news story (version 3 snapshot, `meta_key = narrative:<id>`) and the Operator angle (`operator_angle` and `trigger.kind = operator-angle`) were added the same way. What differs between sources is what the card shows as evidence and why the run started, and that is exactly what the snapshot version and the trigger are for.
- **A kind is a contract with the lifecycle** (this ADR: "Anything that assumed every Proposal has a Narrative, a Tech, a Score and a Spec must read `kind` first"). The Moment idea breaks none of those assumptions that `meme` has not already broken, so it adds nothing for readers of `kind` to learn.
- **The cost of being wrong is low and one-way.** If Moment ideas later need their own lifecycle (for example a different life, because a clip spreads over hours), the source marker (`snapshot.kind = "moment"`) is the field to split on; a migration from "meme with a moment source" to a new kind is a column update, whereas removing a kind that every reader has learned is not.

What the amendment adds, and nothing else:

- **Key and one trio.** A Moment idea is stored under `meta_key = moment:<id>` (like `narrative:<id>`), keyed so it is neither blocked by a Meta's automatic trio nor blocking it. One request for the same Moment is allowed in any 24 hours (the existing duplicate rule), and a Moment never gets an automatic trio unless the full version's switch is on (below).
- **Snapshot version 4.** `{ version: 4, kind: "moment", capturedAt, topic, moment, trigger, trace }`: the topic (label, start time), the Moment as the scanner held it (id, tier, kind and plain subject as the judge wrote them, flags, first and latest flag times), its best posts as they were (X status link, handle, cut text, views, likes, views an hour; links only `x.com` status URLs through `safeHttpUrl`), the trigger (`moment-operator`, with the time), and the coin-side trace (state, the real clones and lookalikes with the figures read). Versions 1 to 3 stay readable; readers branch on `version`.
- **Evidence links** are the Moment's post links and DexScreener pair links, not a Meta's top tokens.
- **Names to avoid** are the traced clones' names and tickers (real clones and lookalikes), plus the topic's Meta's tokens when the topic came from a Meta, so the existing distinct-name rule applies unchanged. The Moment's judge phrases (the words people use for it) are offered to the prompt as the plain names the naming rules ask for.
- **Same safety.** The Moment's posts are third-party text and go into one fenced block under a fresh random boundary, flattened and cut, named untrusted, with the instruction that the ideas are about the Moment outside the fence (the Operator-angle design). The same parser, risk-label rule (a `real person` flag from the judge makes the label required) and legitimacy text apply. Nothing fetches a URL from a post.
- **Trigger and caps.** An Operator press ("Make ideas" on a Moment) writes a request row and nothing else (the War Room still writes no Proposal) and is run by the worker. It counts in the angle allowance (5 in any rolling 24 hours, ceiling 10, one waiting at a time) and in the research budget, not in the automatic 8-a-day cap; it is `capped` or `refused` with the reason, never left waiting. A Moment no longer live when the worker reaches the request ends `refused` ("no longer live") with no model call. The full version (a later ticket) may add an automatic trio for a qualifying `open` Moment under its own switch and its own daily cap (proposed 4), taken from the research budget as the other meme caps are, with the trigger `moment-auto`.
- **No Spec, no Build, no score, 12-hour life, Shortlist or Dismiss:** as for every `meme` Proposal. The 30-a-day new Proposals cap applies as it does to an angle trio.
- **Outcome tracking** (missed-ideas/07) stores the Moment's trace at creation and reads clone coins the same way it does for a Meta's.

What it leaves alone: argus launches nothing and writes text only (ADR 0002); a Moment is not a Source and never starts a Narrative, a judge run or a Tech Proposal (ADR 0011, ADR 0009); the automatic Meta and news paths, their caps and the 12-hour life are unchanged.
