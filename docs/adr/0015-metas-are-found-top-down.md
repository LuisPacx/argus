# Metas are found top-down

Status: accepted (meta-redesign tickets 04 to 07, 2026-11-16; decided by the Operator in `.scratch/cos.md`: "GTA DIAGNOSIS", "GTA USER", "RESEARCH top-down", "USER: all as recommended (Q6 ...)" and "USER: Q12 (a) ... Q13 (a) ..." on 2026-11-16). Supersedes the discovery part of ADR 0014 (how a leader and its subject are found); see "What stays" below. The Operator decided the one open item on 2026-11-16 (Q12 a): one read-only Jupiter path in one module, the ADR 0002 swap ban stays (decision 1, ticket 05 open question 1).

ADR 0014 made a Meta a trend led by a token at millions of market cap, but it still found the leader bottom-up: take the launch themes from pump.fun creates, then look for a big token that carries a theme's name, with DexScreener's paid boosts as the only other candidate list. The GTA VI case (2026-11-16) showed three ways that fails, all read from the live copy:

- **The leader was invisible.** The token `GTA VI ($GTA6)` (`4kjruq...`) stood at about $7M, a bonk launch on Raydium since August, with $355k liquidity. It reached argus once, as a `dexscreener:trending` Signal on 11-07; Signals are stored once (`INSERT OR IGNORE`) and read back by `ingested_at` for 24 h, so it fell out of the candidates the next day. It was not in the top boosts, and launch themes are pump.fun only (the Study marks a bonk create `out-of-scope`). Nothing in ADR 0014 could find it again.
- **The subject was named after one creator.** The key and label of a group are the most frequent by token count. One creator minted `Lucia From GTA 6 ($LUCIA)` 44 times, so the key `lucia` (61) beat `gta6` (21), `gtavi` (17) and `gta vi` (16), and the other GTA clones folded into a group the page called "Lucia From GTA 6" (270 tokens, 156 creators, of which 12 named Lucia).
- **Names that mean one story do not share a word.** Lucia, Vice City and GTA VI are one narrative to a reader and three subjects to the key rule.

Decisions:

1. **Leaders come from what is trending now, not from the launch themes.** Every Meta tick polls two keyless public lists and merges them per token: Jupiter's `toptrending/1h` (`lite-api.jup.ag/tokens/v2/toptrending/1h`: market cap, liquidity, holders, organic score) and GeckoTerminal's Solana `trending_pools` (use `fdv_usd`; `market_cap_usd` is null for most memecoins). A leader may be of any age and any launchpad. **DexScreener is used only to verify:** the candidates that pass the filter get their current cap from the existing `tokenPairs` lookup (ADR 0014, `ARGUS_META_LEADER_CALLS`), which stays the figure the rule uses. DexScreener's boosts are paid ads, not trending (research, 2026-11-16), so they stop being a leader source; the `dexscreener:trending` Signals still exist as Signals (ADR 0011) and the previous Metas' leaders are still looked up first (the carry of ADR 0014). Rate budget: at most 5 requests a tick to the two lists together, retries included (one Jupiter request and up to three GeckoTerminal pages), far under GeckoTerminal's 30 a minute and inside the 5-call window that `lite-api.jup.ag` reports. Birdeye needs a key and pump.fun answers 403 and its API is unofficial, so neither is used; CoinGecko's meme list lags and lacked GTA.
   - **ADR 0002.** `jup.ag` is on the banned-host list because it is also a swap host. The client reads only the public token list (`/tokens/v2/toptrending/`), never a quote, swap, price-for-trading or wallet endpoint, and holds no key. The allowance is the same shape as the Helius amendment: one module, one host, one path prefix, narrowed in `adr-0002.test.ts`, widening needs the Operator's say-so, granted 2026-11-16 (Q12 a) as `lite-api.jup.ag/tokens/v2/*`; this ADR narrows it to the `/tokens/v2/toptrending/` prefix. GeckoTerminal is read the same way (no allowance needed: its host is not banned).
2. **A fake-token filter runs before anything else.** A token is a candidate only when its liquidity is at least $50,000 (`ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_MIN_LIQ_USD`), its market cap is at most 100 times its liquidity (`ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_MAX_CAP_LIQ_RATIO`), and its 24 h volume is at least $50,000 (`ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_MIN_VOLUME_USD`). The evidence: a fake `GTA VI` (`EcLbZ6...`) shows a $12.07B cap on $48M liquidity and $2.28 of 24 h volume (250 times liquidity; the real one is about 26 times), and DexScreener's search returns it beside the real token. Pools of one token are one token (per-token dedupe: the two lists and every pool of a token merge on the mint). Tokenized stocks (the TTWO/GTA6 pool, a Take-Two stock token at $4.6M) are not memecoins and are dropped.
   - **Verification (amended 2026-11-16, Operator Q14 a, after the first live replay).** The candidates that pass are checked on DexScreener's own figures with the same three tests: liquidity and 24 h volume summed over the token's pairs, market cap from the pair with the most liquidity. One exception to "DexScreener's own": the cap-to-liquidity test reads the larger of DexScreener's liquidity and the list's token-level liquidity, but only when the token's Jupiter `organicScore` is at least 50 (`ARGUS_META_TOPDOWN_MIN_ORGANIC`, 0 to 100). Why: DexScreener's token lookup returned one $62k pair for the real GTA VI (cap $7.67M, 123 times that liquidity) while Jupiter showed $271k (organic score 73); read strictly, the rule refused the token it exists to find. A token with no Jupiter score (GeckoTerminal only) or a lower one is judged on DexScreener's liquidity alone, so a list figure cannot rescue a fake; the liquidity and volume minimums are always DexScreener's own.
3. **Copiers count pump.fun and bonk launches.** A subject still needs 10 or more distinct creators in the last 24 h (`ARGUS_META_MIN_COPIES`) to become a Meta or to be Watching, and staying still needs only the leader's $500,000. The count now includes bonk creates (PumpPortal pool `bonk`), which the Study stores as `out-of-scope` and the groups never saw. Only the Meta rule and a Meta's own counts include them; the launch-theme list and its base rate stay pump.fun only (they are what the Study measures).
4. **An AI step groups what trends into named subjects, every 15 minutes.** Different names and tickers on one story are one Meta. Each tick the Brain is given one list: the trending tokens that passed the filter at $1,000,000 or more, and the top 30 launch themes (by creators), each as one short numbered line. It answers one JSON object: subjects, each with a name and the numbers of the items it contains. Rules for the answer, enforced by plain code (the Brain decides nothing else):
   - **It may use only the items it is given.** The model sees ids, never mints; an id that was not in the list, an id used twice, a subject with no id, a name that is empty, over 60 characters or has markup, a URL or a control character refuses the whole answer. It can group and name; it cannot add a token, a theme, a figure or a leader. The leader, the cap and the copiers of a subject are still computed by code from its members.
   - **Token and theme names are untrusted** (written by creators). They go in as data, one cut line each, inside a block that ends only at a boundary line of fresh random bytes, as the summary prompt does; the task has no tools.
   - **Cost.** It is the Brain task `meta-group`, a haiku run with no tools and no API fallback (it waits out a rate limit, like `meta-summary`). It is capped at **$1.00 in any rolling 24 hours** (`ARGUS_META_GROUP_MAX_DAILY_USD`), counted from `brain_runs` with a run that reported no cost charged at its route cap, and it also counts against the research share of the daily quota, like `meta-summary`. 96 ticks a day would pass that cap, so a tick whose input is unchanged (same trending tokens and theme keys as the last good answer, under 60 minutes old) reuses that answer and costs nothing.
   - **Fallback to rules.** With no answer (cap reached, rate limit, API hold, a refused or invalid answer, a failure, `ARGUS_META_GROUP_AI=0`) the tick groups by rules and says so (`namedBy: rules`): the subject's key and label are the ones used by the most **distinct creators**, not tokens (so one creator's 44 mints no longer name a theme), and spelling aliases merge strictly: spaces and roman numerals against digits (`gta 6`, `gta6`, `gta vi`, `gtavi` are one key). "Strict" means a roman numeral of two or more letters (ii to xx) after a base of at least three letters, nothing else (`taxi`, `wifi`, `mix` stay as they are). The rules cannot join names that share nothing (Lucia and Vice City); only the AI step does.
5. **The 14-day re-check is dropped.** The idea was to re-look-up tokens that were trending in the last 14 days against active subjects. With a live trending poll every tick it is not needed: a token that matters is on the list now, and one that was a leader is carried by ADR 0014's previous-leader lookup while it holds $500k.

The meaning of "subject" stays ADR 0014's: the word the Operator uses for what the AI step produces is "narrative", but GLOSSARY keeps **Narrative** for argus's own cluster of Signals, so this ADR, the code and the pages say subject (a Meta's name is the subject's name). A subject's `key` stays a launch-theme group key (the member theme with the most distinct creators), so summaries, matching news, meme Proposals and the carry keep working; the AI step gives the display name, not the key. A subject with no launch-theme member has no copiers and is neither a Meta nor Watching.

What stays from ADR 0014: the thresholds ($1,000,000 to become, $500,000 to stay, at most 3 Metas, Watching the top 3 below $1M, no Watching floor), the hysteresis, staleness and the carry of previous leaders, `meta_current`, New Meta, the summaries, the prompt block and the meme trigger, and the Brain having no part in deciding what is a Meta: the AI step names and groups; becoming, staying and the cap come from plain code on verified numbers. ADR 0014's open question (2), polling the trending feed live, is answered yes, with a different feed.

Costs of this choice. A new dependency on two third-party lists that can change shape or rate-limit: both are read defensively, a failure leaves the tick to the previous leaders and the themes, and none can create a Meta without a DexScreener-verified cap. A grouping model can merge wrongly (a hostile name steering a merge): the guards above bound it to the items given and to creators that really launched, and the War Room shows the merged themes under the name (ticket 07) so a bad merge is visible. Up to $1.00 a day of Brain spend, bounded.

This changes no Narrative, Proposal, Score or Rubric dimension. No wallet, signing or chain call is added (ADR 0002 stands, with the narrow read-only allowance in decision 1).
