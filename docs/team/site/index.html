How it works

From a news story to a live preview of a small product, in seven steps.

  1. Read. Argus reads public news, trend and market feeds all day.
  2. Group. It groups related items into a Narrative: one story that is gaining momentum.
  3. Score. An AI judge scores each Narrative on momentum, novelty, buildability, hook and legitimacy.
  4. Propose. For the best ones, argus proposes a token idea plus a small product (the Tech) that rides the story.
  5. Decide. A human, the Operator, reviews the Proposals in the War Room and approves or dismisses them.
  6. Build. For an approved Proposal, an AI agent builds a small working product inside a locked box.
  7. Preview and launch. The product appears as a preview link. Humans decide whether to launch the token, outside argus.

Highlighted steps are where a person decides.

Small glossary

Signal
One raw item seen in a feed: an article, a trending term, a market event.
Narrative
A cluster of recent Signals about one subject that is gaining momentum.
Proposal
A Narrative plus a token angle (name, ticker) and a Tech worth building. What the Operator decides on.
Tech
The small product argus can build that rides the Narrative.
Spec
The written brief for a Build. The Operator edits it before approving.
Build
The small working product made from an approved Proposal's Spec.
Operator
The one person who approves each gate.
War Room
The local dashboard where the Operator reviews Proposals.

Guardrails

What keeps this safe: four rules, plus one.

Human in the loop

A person decides at every gate: shortlist, approve the Spec, dismiss, launch. Argus never launches a token and holds no wallet.

Containment

Builds run in a locked container: no passwords or keys inside, internet only to two allowed sites, no access to the Operator's files. There is no uncontained mode.

Budget caps

Every AI run has a spend cap. Research is held to about 30% of the daily plan. A Build gets a $25 cap and 4 hours. Hitting a cap stops the run; caps are never raised silently.

Subscription only

AI work runs on the Claude subscription, not pay-per-use. The optional paid fallback is off, and capped at $0.50 a day if ever turned on.

Honesty

Every Build carries a footer saying argus made it and the creator may sell. Argus does no posting, botting or promotion.

  • Research and Builds share the plan limits with the Operator's own use.
